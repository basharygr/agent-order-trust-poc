# Agent enablement on e-commerce platforms other than Shopify (as of 3 November 2026)

Scope: what non-Shopify platforms ship so that AI shopping agents (protocol/API agents and browser-driven agents) can read a merchant's catalog, inventory, pricing, shipping eligibility and checkout. Every claim is dated; "vendor claim" vs "third-party evidence" is flagged. Where no documentation exists this is stated as a gap rather than guessed.

Important landscape context that colours every platform below (all dated):
- OpenAI retired ChatGPT "Instant Checkout" in March 2026 and moved purchasing to merchant Apps inside ChatGPT; ACP continues as the discovery/feed infrastructure. OpenAI spokesperson (6 Mar 2026): "Instant Checkout is moving to Apps, where purchases can happen more seamlessly" — [Digital Commerce 360, 6 Mar 2026](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/03/06/openai-shifts-checkout-plans-agentic-commerce-strategy/). OpenAI's own developer docs now say external checkout is required and that purchases complete on the merchant's own domain; "Instant Checkout inside ChatGPT is only available to select marketplace partners" — [Checkout.com docs summarising OpenAI requirements](https://www.checkout.com/docs/payments/accept-payments/accept-a-payment-via-agentic-commerce/accept-a-payment-via-chatgpt).
- Google's UCP (announced 11 Jan 2026) is the live in-chat checkout path: Universal Cart launched in the US on 15 Oct 2026 across Search/AI Mode and the Gemini app, merchant stays merchant of record, Google Pay rails — [Google blog, 15 Oct 2026](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/shopping/universal-cart-launch/); [Digital Commerce 360, 16 Oct 2026](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/10/16/google-universal-cart-launch-features/). Checkout is US-only; Canada/Australia announced for early 2027 per third-party tracker — [sanbi.ai tracker, 23 Oct 2026](https://sanbi.ai/blog/agentic-commerce-tracker-ai-checkout).
- Microsoft Copilot Checkout launched 8 Jan 2026 (US), rails via PayPal, Shopify, Stripe; Stripe/PayPal merchants apply, Shopify merchants auto-enrolled with opt-out window — [Retail TouchPoints, Jan 2026](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/news/etsy-shopify-among-early-partners-for-microsofts-copilot-checkout/156346/); [Axios, 8 Jan 2026](https://www.axios.com/2026/01/08/microsoft-ai-copilot-checkout).
- Browser-driven agents: WebMCP (Google-proposed, early preview) is the emerging standard; Commerce.com/BigCommerce (21 Oct 2026) and PayPal Store Sync (16 Sep 2026) ship it; OpenAI, Google, Microsoft enabling it in ChatGPT desktop app, Chrome and Edge per PayPal — [PayPal developer blog, 16 Sep 2026](https://developer.paypal.com/community/blog/WebMCP_PayPal_Agent_Ready/).
- Independent census (UCP Checker, 30 Oct 2026): >19,000 valid UCP profiles across ~25,000 domains checked; Shopify ~10,950, Wix ~6,700, Jumpseller 450, Shoptet 335, Tray 245, Boutir 175, BigCommerce 65, Shopware 38, WooCommerce ~20, Magento/Cafe24 a handful, VTEX/PrestaShop/Tiendanube/1C-Bitrix 1 each, Salesforce Commerce Cloud 1 of 77 checked — [UCP Checker, State of Agentic Commerce Oct 2026](https://ucpchecker.com/blog/state-of-agentic-commerce-october-2026). Third-party measurement; counts cover only domains the tool checks.

## Key Question 1: Stripe Agentic Commerce Suite — exact scope as of Nov 2026, live platforms, what a WooCommerce/Wix merchant gets, geography, data fidelity, controls, volume disclosures

### Takeaway
Stripe ACS (announced 11 Dec 2025, expanded at Sessions 29 Apr 2026) is a Stripe-hosted catalog feed + Stripe-managed checkout + Shared Payment Tokens, syndicated to agents (ChatGPT via ACP, Google AI Mode/Gemini via UCP, Copilot, Meta ads); Stripe-managed checkout is documented as available in the US, Canada and "select European countries", merchants pick agents in the Dashboard and can mark products discovery-only. Platform partners (Wix, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Squarespace, commercetools) are all "rolling out"/waitlist/eligible-merchant language; Stripe has published no merchant counts, GMV, conversion rates or fees for ACS.

### Cited Findings
- Announced 11 Dec 2025; components: hosted ACP endpoint for product/price/availability, Checkout built on Checkout Sessions API with optional tax and shipping handling, Shared Payment Tokens (SPTs) "scoped to a specific seller, bounded by time and amount", Radar risk relay, order events; onboarding = join waitlist, upload catalog to Stripe or connect via syndicator, select agents in Dashboard. Merchant remains merchant of record and keeps refunds/disputes. Platform partners: Wix, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Squarespace, commercetools; omnichannel partners Akeneo, Cymbio, Logicbroker, Mirakl, Pipe17, Rithum. Named early merchants: URBN, Etsy, Ashley Furniture, Coach, Kate Spade, Nectar, Revolve, Halara, Abt. Stripe claims building ACP integrations independently "can take up to six months per agent". No GA date, geography, fees or volume stated — [Stripe blog, 11 Dec 2025](https://stripe.com/blog/agentic-commerce-suite); [Stripe newsroom, 11 Dec 2025](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/agentic-commerce-suite).
- Stripe Sessions (29 Apr 2026): Google partnership — customers "will soon be able to buy your products in AI Mode and the Gemini app" via UCP; Meta partnership for native checkout inside Facebook ads; Link agent wallet; Issuing for agents (preview); Radar for agents (preview); Machine Payments Protocol with Tempo; platforms can preview ACS so connected accounts become agent-ready. No agentic merchant counts, volumes or fees given — [Stripe, Everything we announced at Sessions 2026](https://stripe.com/blog/everything-we-announced-at-sessions-2026).
- Third-party recap of Sessions: Wix, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Squarespace, commercetools described as "day-one" platform support; Stripe joined UCP Tech Council 24 Apr 2026 alongside Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, Salesforce; Stripe claims 1 in 6 attempted signups across AI services come from bad actors — [Stellagent, 30 Apr 2026](https://stellagent.ai/insights/stripe-agentic-commerce-suite-google-integration) (secondary; cites Stripe blog).
- Current Stripe docs (fetched 3 Nov 2026): "With Agentic Commerce Suite, Stripe manages the whole flow by default. You share a catalog feed, and Stripe shares your products with agents, manages checkout, processes payments, and notifies you of orders." Stripe-managed checkout "is available in the US, Canada, and select European countries." Hooks let merchants approve orders, calculate taxes/shipping, provide real-time prices and inventory; "handling every checkout event is in private preview in the US." Merchants may alternatively "run your own Universal Commerce Protocol checkout and accept payments with the Stripe Payments handler." A separate guide exists for Connect platforms ("enable in-context selling on AI agents") — [Stripe docs, Sell products through agents](https://docs.stripe.com/agentic-commerce/sellers/use-cases/retail.md).
- Stripe docs landing: "Availability, launch stage, and supported countries vary by integration"; sellers can sell retail, service bookings, or charge for tool calls/HTTP via MPP and x402; agents can use Link Agent Wallet, embedded commerce, or WebMCP in browser — [Stripe docs, Agentic commerce](https://docs.stripe.com/agentic-commerce).
- Data fidelity (Stripe catalog feed spec, fetched 3 Nov 2026): CSV via Product Catalog Import API; each row = product or variant; required fields id, title, description, link, brand (most categories), image_link, availability (in_stock/out_of_stock/preorder/backorder), price; variants via item_group_id + color/size/size_system/gender + up to 3 custom variant options; inventory_quantity required unless inventory_not_tracked; shipping is required for shippable goods, expressed per country:delivery_area(region or US-only postal codes):service:speed_range:price, plus shipping_exclusions and free_shipping_threshold (regional eligibility is thus expressible at country/region level, postal-code level only for US); regulatory applicable_fees by country/region; tax via Stripe Product Tax Code or Anrok code; optional return_rate, review counts/ratings, Q&A, highlights, related products, product_warning (prop_65 etc.), age_restriction. Separate incremental inventory, price and promotion feeds (promo codes, percent/amount off, free shipping). `disable_checkout=true` makes a product discovery-only (agent redirects to merchant site). Stripe "validate[s] and clean[s] your data, index[es] it into the Stripe product feed, and convert[s] it to the format each agent requires". No returns-policy field is in the spec (only return_rate metric). Freshness is merchant-driven ("refresh your feed frequently"); no SLA stated — [Stripe docs, Catalog feed](https://docs.stripe.com/agentic-commerce/concepts/catalog-feed.md).
- OpenAI side of ACP (current docs): merchants send a regularly refreshed CSV/JSON product feed (identifiers, descriptions, pricing, inventory, media, fulfillment); initial sample validated, then daily snapshots; checkout state and payment processing happen on merchant systems; OpenAI creates a one-time delegated payment request with max amount and expiry; Stripe SPT is the first compatible implementation, more PSPs "coming soon"; OpenAI is not merchant of record — [OpenAI commerce docs, Key concepts](https://developers.openai.com/commerce/guides/key-concepts).
- Stripe expanded agentic payments to Affirm and Klarna (4 Mar 2026 headline referenced) — [Digital Commerce 360, 6 Mar 2026](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/03/06/openai-shifts-checkout-plans-agentic-commerce-strategy/) (headline only; content not retrieved).
- What a WooCommerce merchant gets: WooCommerce's own post (11 Dec 2025) says ACS "will be available to WooCommerce users through the official Stripe extension", merchants "join the waitlist", rollout "in the coming months"; no agents named, no fee, no country, no order-sync detail — [WooCommerce blog, 11 Dec 2025](https://woocommerce.com/posts/stripe-agentic-commerce-suite-for-woocommerce/). Commerce-matrix tracker lists WooCommerce ACP and UCP as "In Progress", MCP "Supported" as of Oct 2026 — [agenticplug.ai, updated 31 Oct 2026](https://agenticplug.ai/current-state-of-agentic-commerce) (third-party tracker).
- What a Wix merchant gets: Wix press release (11 Dec 2025) — "eligible Wix merchants", "gradual basis", "single, low-code setup"; no countries, plans, agents, fees or data detail; Wix Payments co-heads call it "Stripe's Agentic Storefront" — [Wix press room, 11 Dec 2025](https://www.wix.com/press-room/home/post/wix-partners-with-stripe-to-bring-agentic-commerce-suite-to-eligible-wix-merchants-in-the-agentic-co). Wix blog (updated 26 Oct 2026): eligible merchants "get access automatically, and the dashboard shows status", Wix Payments handles the work, rollout gradual; checkout location "varies" (in AI interface or back to Wix store); no order-attribution feature described beyond an "AI Visibility Overview" showing citation frequency/sentiment; no fees — [Wix blog, Agentic commerce on Wix](https://www.wix.com/blog/agentic-commerce-on-wix).
- commercetools documents a Stripe ACS integration via its AI Hub ("Stripe Agentic Commerce Suite overview") — [commercetools docs](https://docs.commercetools.com/agentic-commerce/ai-hub/stripe/overview). JD Sports is "the first enterprise retailer to deploy ACS" (12 Jan 2026), with commercetools AI Hub keeping product data, pricing, availability in sync and Stripe handling payments/fraud; markets, agents and timing not specified — [commercetools press release via PR Newswire, 12 Jan 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/commercetools-kicks-off-nrf-2026--including-updates-on-stripe-acs-ai-hub-and-helping-enterprises-stay-discoverable-and-shoppable-in-agentic-commerce-302658128.html).
- BigCommerce added Stripe ACS alongside PayPal "to give merchants more choice" (Dec 2025) — [Digital Transactions](https://www.digitaltransactions.net/visa-predicts-agentic-commerce-will-be-mainstream-in-2026-bigcommerce-adds-stripes-agentic-commerce-suite/).
- Squarespace: SVP Dan Chandre quoted in Stripe launch that merchants "will be able to effortlessly bring their products into AI agents"; no Squarespace help-center article found confirming live availability, plan tiers or setup — [Stripe newsroom](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/agentic-commerce-suite); a third-party review (undated 2026) says the Stripe/Squarespace path is "still a waitlist" — [Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/best-agentic-commerce-tools) (low-authority).
- Volume evidence (third-party): Forrester analyst Emily Pfeiffer estimated only ~30 merchants were actually live and transacting on agent checkout as of Feb 2026 (vs "1 million+ Shopify merchants" eligibility figure) — reported in [beancount.io guide, 29 Jul 2026](https://beancount.io/blog/2026/07/29/ai-shopping-agents-agentic-commerce-protocol-guide) (secondary citation of Forrester; not verified against Forrester directly). Also: "about a dozen"/"roughly 30" Shopify merchants went live on Instant Checkout before retirement, per various secondary guides — [Contra Collective](https://contracollective.com/blog/agentic-commerce-protocol-stripe-openai-checkout-standard-2026); [ekamoira](https://www.ekamoira.com/blog/chatgpt-instant-checkout-agentic-commerce-protocol-2026) (conflicting counts; both secondary).

### Inferences
- For a WooCommerce or Wix SMB today, ACS practically means: a Stripe-formatted catalog feed syndicated for discovery in ChatGPT (and, "soon", Google AI Mode/Gemini via UCP and Copilot), with Stripe-managed checkout where the agent surface supports it; since ChatGPT no longer does in-chat checkout for non-marketplace merchants, the realistic in-chat purchase path for an ACS merchant is Google AI Mode/Gemini (US-only shoppers) or Copilot (US), with ChatGPT acting as discovery + redirect to the product `link`.
- Geography: Stripe-managed checkout is limited to US, Canada and select EU countries; a merchant outside those (e.g., Israel) can at best use discovery-only syndication or build their own UCP checkout. Stripe has not published the EU country list.
- The absence of any Stripe-published ACS merchant count, GMV or conversion data 11 months after launch, combined with third-party estimates of ~30 live checkout merchants in Feb 2026, suggests actual agent-checkout volume through ACS remains small and undisclosed.

### Gaps
- No Stripe-published ACS fee schedule beyond standard processing; no published list of "select European countries".
- No official Wix, WooCommerce or Squarespace documentation stating what "eligible" means (country, plan, payment provider), whether the feature is default-on or opt-in beyond "automatic for eligible merchants" (Wix), or how agent orders are labelled in the merchant's order list.
- No Stripe disclosure of ACS merchant counts, agent GMV, conversion or SPT volumes as of 3 Nov 2026.
- Whether ACS catalog data includes a merchant returns policy (the feed spec has no such field) is unresolved.

## Key Question 3: WooCommerce and Wix natively (plus Squarespace, Shopware, PrestaShop, Webflow, Lightspeed/Ecwid, marketplaces) — plugins, MCP servers, feeds, bot handling

### Takeaway
Wix is the only non-Shopify SMB platform that publishes agent-usable UCP profiles (catalog, cart, checkout, fulfillment, discount) by default across ~6,700 census stores, but payment always escalates to the Wix checkout page and version lags (2026-04-08); WooCommerce core has an opt-in developer-preview MCP (products + orders only, no cart/checkout) and relies on Stripe ACS or third-party plugins for ACP/UCP; Shopware ships a free official UCP/MCP/feed plugin (beta, v1.4.0, 2 Oct 2026) with poor reviews; PrestaShop has an official MCP module but no UCP/ACP; Squarespace, Webflow, Lightspeed/Ecwid have no documented native agent enablement.

### Cited Findings

#### WooCommerce (Automattic)

### Inferences
- Platforms with no native enablement (Squarespace in practice, Webflow, Lightspeed/Ecwid, Magento Open Source, PrestaShop beyond MCP, most regional platforms) are reachable by agents only via (a) feed syndication (OpenAI feed spec, Google Merchant Center, PayPal Store Sync, Feedonomics), (b) no-integration universal checkout (Rye, Crossmint, browser agents), or (c) edge injection (Cloudflare WebMCP, currently only documented for Commerce.com).
- The no-merchant-integration pattern (Rye/Crossmint) effectively treats the storefront as a browser target; merchant controls, caps, exclusions and attribution are absent by construction, and bot-management (Cloudflare/Vercel defaults) determines whether these agents succeed.
- Pricing opacity is universal: none of Firmly, Rye, Crossmint, PayOS, PayPal Agent Ready, Adyen Agentic, Checkout.com agentic or Stripe ACS publishes fees beyond standard processing.

### Gaps
- No published pricing for Firmly, Rye, Crossmint, Nevermined, PayOS, PayPal Agent Ready, Adyen Agentic or Checkout.com agentic.
- No independent traction data for Firmly ("11M+ merchants reachable" is vendor claim), Rye, or Crossmint World Store coverage.
- Cloudflare WebMCP availability for individual (non-Commerce.com) merchants and its pricing tier undocumented; Cloudflare solutions page content not retrievable.
- PayPal has disclosed no agentic volumes; current status of PayPal "Agent Ready" in ChatGPT/Gemini (announced "coming soon" Jan 2026) unconfirmed.
