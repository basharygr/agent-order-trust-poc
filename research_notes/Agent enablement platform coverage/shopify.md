# Shopify agent enablement: inventory of agent-facing capabilities as of 3 November 2026

Scope note: all findings dated; "Shopify claim" = Shopify marketing/earnings/help-center statement; "third-party" or "merchant" = non-Shopify evidence. Shopify's Q3 2026 results were scheduled for release before market open on 3 Nov 2026 but were not retrievable from Shopify IR, the Shopify newsroom, SEC EDGAR search, or news search at time of writing (see Gaps under Key Question 4).

## Key Question 1: Protocol/API pattern — what each agent channel exposes, freshness, coverage, checkout location, seller of record, analytics

### Takeaway
Shopify's agent stack is one product-data layer (Shopify Catalog, >1B products, default-on for every eligible Starter-or-higher store) syndicated to named channels (ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Mode/Gemini, Meta/Muse, Shop, "Other channels") plus a UCP/MCP checkout layer (Checkout MCP, Checkout WebMCP, Shop Pay inside agents). Direct (in-agent) checkout exists only for Google, Copilot and Meta and only for buyers in a handful of countries (US; plus CA/AU for Google; plus CA/MX/BR/JP/SG for Meta); ChatGPT is referral-only to the merchant's own checkout. The merchant remains seller of record everywhere; freshness is described as "real-time"/"continuously updated" with no published SLA; per-channel analytics exist in the Agentic admin section but not for headless stores.

### Cited Findings

**Shopify Catalog (the data layer)**
- Catalog is the product data source AI channels use; products published to the online store are discoverable on Shop via Catalog; variants can be grouped into single listings; if Catalog access is off, products can still be found through web crawling/indexing — [Shopify Help Center, Agentic storefronts home](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/agentic-home)
- Eligibility: store must be on "the Starter plan or higher"; not password-protected/private mode; Shopify Payments account (if used) in good standing; ToS/AUP compliance; account standing (verified email, 2FA, genuine sales, prompt fulfilment, low chargeback rate, invoices paid). Product: title, at least one image, price > 0, published to Online Store/Hydrogen/Headless, identifiable URL, not Unlisted/hidden from search, no sensitive content, not in excluded categories. "Eligibility is reviewed over time." Agentic-plan stores without a Shopify online store must supply an external product URL. No country restriction is stated on this page — [Shopify Help Center, Shopify Catalog requirements](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-catalog/requirements)
- Excluded categories (no agent exposure at all): alcohol, tobacco/vape, drugs & smoking accessories incl. CBD, prescription/medical products, weapons/ammo, imitation weapons, explosives, hazardous materials, adult/mature, sexual health, money-related (bullion, virtual currency), prop money, services and add-ons (warranties, subscription services), event tickets, live animals, uncategorized products; subcategories inherit exclusion; Shopify may change the list — [Shopify Help Center, Shopify Catalog requirements](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-catalog/requirements)
- An ineligibility reason exists for "Store is opted out of sharing products with AI channels — contact Shopify Support (separate from the Shopify Catalog access setting)", i.e., a support-mediated full opt-out status exists but is undocumented as a self-serve control — [Shopify Help Center, Shopify Catalog requirements](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-catalog/requirements)
- Default listing data: title, description, options, images, price, availability and "other key attributes"; "Shopify Catalog continuously updates inventory and prices"; merchants can map title/description/category from metafields, metaobjects, tag prefixes, title delimiters; compare-at price can be shared and merchant chooses whether list price = current or compare-at — [Shopify Help Center, Agentic storefronts: products](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/products)
- B2B handling: B2B-only products excluded (detected via B2B catalogs, customer-account requirements, password-protected storefronts); custom B2B setups via apps/themes "may not be detected" and could appear; agentic storefronts are D2C-only — products sold to both B2B and D2C expose the D2C price, "B2B pricing isn't shown" — [Shopify Help Center, Agentic storefronts: products](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/products)
- Global Catalog MCP (developer-facing, UCP endpoint `/api/ucp/mcp`): tools `search_catalog`, `lookup_catalog` (up to 50 IDs/URLs), `get_product`. Buyer context inputs: `address_country`, `address_region`, `postal_code`, `language`, `currency`, `intent`. Filters: `available` (default true = sale-ready only), `ships_to` (country/region/postal), `ships_from`, `price` min/max, `condition`, `shops`, `attributes`, `rating`, `price_tier`, `media`, `categories`. Variant fields returned: `sku`, `title`, `price`, `list_price`, `checkout_url`, `condition`, `eligible.native_checkout`, `availability` (`available`, `status`, `running_low`), `requires` (shipping, selling_plan, components), `options`, `tags`, `seller`. Seller links can include a `refund_policy` URL. Not covered in the doc: shipping rates, returns policy content, promotions, member/B2B pricing, freshness/update frequency, rate limits. Results capped at 1,000; `total_count` is an estimate — [Shopify Dev, Global Catalog MCP](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/catalog/global-catalog)
- Shopify-generated/enriched fields (`description`, `options`, `metadata.attributes`, `metadata.tech_specs`, `metadata.top_features`, `metadata.unique_selling_points`, `variants[].condition`) are to be treated as "discovery and merchandising signals," not merchant-authored text — [Shopify Dev, Global Catalog MCP](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/catalog/global-catalog)
- Freshness: Shopify describes Catalog as continuously updated/real-time; "no contractual freshness SLA (in seconds or minutes) is published"; search defaults to available, sale-ready products — [Retailgentic, Part 2, 25 Aug 2026](https://www.retailgentic.com/p/shopify-and-agentic-commerce-part-844)
- Clustering/canonicalisation under a Universal Product ID groups variants across stores; in an Olaplex 3 example, 3.4 oz and 8.5 oz sizes with very different prices were grouped, which the author suspects is mis-canonicalisation; inferred content accuracy "varies, and merchants can't fully control it"; no human review of inferred categories/clustering is described — [Retailgentic, Part 2, 25 Aug 2026](https://www.retailgentic.com/p/shopify-and-agentic-commerce-part-844)
- April 2026: Storefront Catalog MCP migrated to UCP (endpoint, tool-name and schema changes) — [Retailgentic, Part 2, 25 Aug 2026](https://www.retailgentic.com/p/shopify-and-agentic-commerce-part-844)
- Fall '26 (28 Oct 2026) Catalog API additions: media-type filters, sale prices, safety warnings, markdown details; Catalog search of off-Shopify products is in beta; compare-at prices can appear to agents "with no extra setup"; promoted placement in early access (developers earn the Shop Campaigns commission plus 0.3% from Shopify) — [Shopify Editions Fall '26](https://www.shopify.com/editions/fall2026)
- Agentic plan: rolled out "globally" (Shopify wording, 24 Mar 2026) for brands not on Shopify to be listed in Catalog — [Shopify News, "agentic commerce momentum", 24 Mar 2026](https://shopify.com/news/agentic-commerce-momentum)

### Inferences
- Shopify's "every merchant is UCP-ready" is accurate at the data/endpoint level but not at the transaction level: in-agent checkout is gated by channel (3 of 6), buyer country (US-centric; max six countries on Meta), and product type (no subscriptions, bundles, customizable, B2B, local delivery/pickup).
- The Catalog API exposes availability and a `ships_to` filter, but no shipping rates, no returns-policy content (only a URL), no promotions and no member/B2B pricing — so an agent cannot compute landed cost or regional deliverability pre-checkout from Catalog alone; that information only materialises inside a Shopify checkout session.
- "Real-time" freshness is asserted but unmeasured; the 7-day lag for Catalog removal, the 1,000-result cap and the estimate-only `total_count` suggest an indexed/syndicated system, not live reads.
- The Supplemental Terms place all transaction liability on the merchant while Shopify disclaims the channels' behaviour; no dispute-evidence mechanism for agent-placed orders exists in Shopify's documentation.

### Gaps
- Shopify does not publish a freshness SLA, agent-order error/cancellation rates, or absolute agentic order counts/GMV.
- No Shopify document states which plan tiers see the Agentic analytics (beyond Starter+ Catalog eligibility); Basic-plan-specific limitations are undocumented.
- Whether Perplexity is currently a Catalog consumer is contradicted across secondary sources; Shopify's help pages do not name it.
- No primary Shopify source for the Sept 2026 Claude/Shop Pay announcement was retrievable; only the 28 Oct 2026 Fall '26 posts.
- Fee amounts under Supplemental Terms Section 6.1 ("certain Participating Channels... may assess a fee") are not published; Finkelstein said "no new fees" — unresolved tension.

## Key Question 2: Merchant controls — toggles, global opt-out, product-level exclusions, caps, agent identity, Fall '26 changes, post-Q3 changes

### Takeaway
Controls are a master "Allow Shopify to manage for me" switch (default on), beneath which sit a global Catalog-access toggle (does not cover Shop), an auto-enroll toggle, and per-channel Catalog-access and/or Direct-checkout toggles; product-level exclusion is only via Unlisted/`seo.hidden`, which also removes the product from Google and store search. There are no price caps, quantity caps, per-market agent rules, or agent-identity requirements on the merchant side; the only documented full opt-out is a support ticket. Fall '26 added a merchant-approved "knowledge base" of store facts and an eligibility view; nothing new is documented at or after Q3 results on 3 Nov 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Default state: "Allow Shopify to manage for me" on; all channels can access products; direct checkout active where supported; auto-enrolment in future storefronts. Turning it off unlocks: global Shopify Catalog access (removes third-party access, "doesn't remove products from Shop"); "Auto enroll new storefronts"; per-channel Direct checkout (Google, Copilot, Meta); per-channel Catalog access (ChatGPT, Copilot, Meta); "Other channels" group toggle. Turning the master switch back on "reactivates all settings, including direct checkout" — [Shopify Help Center, Agentic storefronts home](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/agentic-home)
- Access to the Agentic section requires store owner or staff with "App development > View apps developed by staff and collaborators" or "App and sales channel > Agentic" — [Shopify Help Center, Agentic storefronts home](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/agentic-home)
- Product-level: Unlisted status or `seo.hidden` metafield hides from all agentic storefronts incl. Shop, but also from sitemaps, search engines and online-store search; `robots.txt.liquid` rules are "advisory, and blocking them doesn't stop Shopify Catalog syndication"; Shopify advises against a proxy in front of Shopify — [Shopify Help Center, Agentic storefronts: products](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/products)
- Removal latency: up to 7 days after Catalog access is turned off — [Shopify Help Center, ChatGPT](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/chatgpt)
- Supplemental Terms: "it is your responsibility to disable the features for that Participating Channel" in admin; Shopify may terminate "for any reason and at any time without notice" — [Shopify, Agentic Storefronts Supplemental Terms, updated 6 Oct 2026](https://www.shopify.com/legal/terms-agentic-storefronts)
- Timeline of defaults: 12 Jan 2026 Shopify first opted merchants in automatically; 24 Mar 2026 Agentic Storefronts live for all eligible merchants ("discoverable in ChatGPT by default"); May 2026 Agentic admin section; 8 Sep 2026 Meta auto-added — [Search Engine Journal, 2 Oct 2026](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/shopify-now-enrolls-your-store-in-every-new-ai-shopping-channel/591049/); [Shopify News, 24 Mar 2026](https://shopify.com/news/agentic-commerce-momentum); [Motley Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/12/shopify-shop-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)
- Merchant complaint, 4 Apr 2026 (thread "Not allowed to Opt-out of Generative AI 'Agentic Storefronts'", 10 replies): channel enabled without opt-in; support only suggested Unlisted; one merchant (ImTired) got the channel removed via a support removal request confirmed 29 Apr 2026; no Shopify staff replies; concerns about AI training on images, copyright, loss of creative customers — [Shopify Community thread 600746](https://community.shopify.com/t/not-allowed-to-opt-out-of-generative-ai-agentic-storefronts/600746)

- No price/quantity caps, per-market agent rules or agent-identity (allowlist) requirements exist in the merchant-facing controls documented on the agentic-home, products, ChatGPT, Microsoft, Google or Meta pages — [Shopify Help Center, Agentic storefronts home](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/agentic-home); [products](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/products)

## Key Question 3: Browsing pattern — how storefronts treat browser-driven agents (Muse, Comet, Claude in Chrome, ChatGPT agent)

### Takeaway
Shopify has moved to welcome browser agents rather than block them: unsigned agents get the strictest rate limits (from 30 May 2026), Web Bot Auth-signed agents get higher limits and are the only ones Shopify verifies for Checkout WebMCP; every standard Liquid storefront serves `/agents.md` (with `/llms.txt` mirroring it), `/.well-known/ucp` and an MCP endpoint; and a 250-store study found Claude-driven agents were stopped on 0 of 54 Shopify stores versus 73% of non-Shopify stores. Headless/Hydrogen stores get none of the agent files by default, merchant-level bot protection is a Plus-only flash-sale feature whose interaction with agents is undocumented, and merchants report theme-level breakage (cookie walls, icon-only buttons, JS-rendered prices, unreadable variant selectors).

### Cited Findings
- Shopify changelog (posted 7 May 2026, effective 30 May 2026): stricter rate limits for bots and agents on the Storefront API and Shopify-hosted online store HTML pages; unsigned traffic gets the strictest limits; Web Bot Auth-signed traffic gets higher limits; higher tiers by request form; merchants can get ready-to-use Web Bot Auth signatures in admin to crawl their own stores — [Shopify Dev changelog, Web Bot Auth](https://shopify.dev/changelog/bots-and-agents-should-identify-themselves-via-web-bot-auth)
- Checkout WebMCP (UCP version 2026-08-25): browser agents sign requests with Web Bot Auth headers (`Signature-Agent`, `Signature-Input`, `Signature`); "Shopify verifies only registered WBA keys" (Ed25519 key directory registered/published with Shopify); unsigned requests "may be deprioritized or blocked"; tools `get_checkout`, `update_checkout` (PUT semantics; ignores `line_items`), `complete_checkout` (no params), `navigate_to_storefront`; registered only on "eligible checkouts"; payment limited to saved Shop Pay cards ("doesn't accept new card details"), other payment methods chosen by buyer on page; the store must ship to the address or checkout rejects it; buyer must see order and total and approve before `complete_checkout`; "WBA and `ready_for_complete` status don't count as permission"; only `status: completed` confirms; `completion_failed` means do not resubmit; tool responses may contain prompt-injection text; Chrome 153 rejects object arguments, Chrome 155 plans to deprecate JSON strings — [Shopify Dev, Checkout WebMCP](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/carts-and-checkout/checkout-webmcp)
- Changelog ~late Sept 2026: WebMCP support extended to checkout for browser agents — [Shopify Dev changelog, WebMCP support for checkout](https://shopify.dev/changelog/posts/webmcp-support-for-checkout); covered by [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/shopify-extends-webmcp-support-to-checkout-for-ai-agents)

- `/agents.md` is the canonical agent-discovery file served by `agents.md.liquid`; `/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt` fall back to it unless dedicated templates exist; default content lists product JSON endpoints (`/products/{handle}.json`) and the sitemap — [Shopify Dev, agents.md.liquid](https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/themes/architecture/templates/agents-md-liquid)
- A merchant thread claims Shopify "killed /llms.txt for all stores" (redirect); commenters say most stores just mirror `/agents.md` — [Shopify Community thread 629150](https://community.shopify.com/t/shopify-just-killed-llms-txt-for-all-stores-heres-what-to-do/629150)
- Live test (2 Nov 2026, three large standard stores + headless stores + one dev store): standard storefronts return 200 for `/agents.md` and `/llms.txt`, a JSON `/.well-known/ucp` profile naming the MCP endpoint, and `/api/ucp/mcp` answering `tools/list`; `robots.txt` points agents to `/agents.md` and `/.well-known/ucp`; headless storefronts return 404 for agent files and publish no UCP profile; the UCP profile responded even on a password-protected dev store; app-proxy paths (`/apps/...`) returned 403 to GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot but 200 to OAI-SearchBot and Chrome; Shopify doesn't expose request logs to apps; JSON-LD not tested — [DEV Community, 2 Nov 2026](https://dev.to/abhaygawade/shopify-agentsmd-and-llmstxt-what-we-found-testing-real-stores-okb)
- Third-party study (GitHub, Claude agents, 250 US stores, Oct 2026): Shopify stores stopped the agent in 0 of 54 cases; non-Shopify stores stopped it in 66 of 90 (73%); 60 of 84 stops happened on homepage/first click and 7 at checkout; 22 stops showed no CAPTCHA/block page; author attributes blocks to automated-browser detection — [GitHub, d-walia/agent-checkout-study](https://github.com/d-walia/agent-checkout-study) (repo page returned 404 on direct fetch; figures from search snippet — treat as unverified)

- Merchant tests of the Sept 2026 Shopify/Anthropic reference agent (thread opened 3 Sep 2026, replies to 25 Sep): nobody reported a completed agent checkout; browser-agent stalls: icon-only add-to-cart buttons without accessible names, JS-rendered prices, cookie wall swallowing first click, variant selection not machine-readable, password/"coming soon" mode; data issues: two conflicting prices in markup, availability contradicting the button, variant-level stock hidden behind "in stock" parent, market/currency mismatches, UCP endpoints blocked by CDN bot rules; UCP path (`/.well-known/ucp`, `/api/ucp/mcp`, 13 tools) never touches the theme DOM; on 24 Sep 2026 four stores returned identical UCP profiles with nine capabilities and no loyalty extension — "agents can't see member status or member pricing"; no Shopify staff replies — [Shopify Community thread 675870](https://community.shopify.com/t/shopify-just-released-an-ai-shopping-agent-that-shops-your-store-through-ucp-has-anyone-tested-whether-it-can-actually-check-out-on-theirs/675870)

- No test specifically of Perplexity Comet, Claude in Chrome or ChatGPT agent on Shopify storefronts was found — [search summary](https://dev.to/artemkireev/ai-shopping-agents-at-checkout-allow-block-or-verify-bne)

### Inferences
- On Shopify, the browsing pattern is effectively permitted by default and merchants have no self-serve control over it; the only gating is platform-level (rate limits for unsigned agents; WBA key registration for Checkout WebMCP tools).
- Agent-readability varies by theme, not by platform: the same store can expose a clean UCP/MCP path while its DOM defeats a browser agent (cookie walls, inaccessible buttons, hidden variant stock). This is a per-store quality problem Shopify does not audit.
- Headless/Hydrogen merchants are second-class for agents: no agent files, no UCP profile, no Agentic analytics.
- Checkout WebMCP's payment limitation (saved Shop Pay cards only) means browser-agent checkout on Shopify is practically a Shop Pay funnel.

- No Shopify data on how many agent checkouts fail at the DOM level; failed agent checkouts are invisible in admin (merchant observation).
- JSON-LD/variant-level structured data quality on Shopify themes was not measured by any source found.
- The 250-store GitHub study could not be fetched directly; its numbers are from a search snippet only.

### Inferences
- The highest-leverage third-party gaps for small merchants are (a) a pre-checkout "transactability" verdict per variant per destination (ships-to, landed cost, returns window, promo validity, stock confidence age) that Shopify's Catalog does not emit; (b) a consent/evidence record for agent-placed orders usable in disputes, which neither Shopify's terms nor its checkout tooling provide; (c) honest attribution that counts failed agent checkouts and direct-checkout orders that bypass pixels; (d) rule-based gating (per product, market, channel, quantity, price drift) beyond Shopify's binary toggles.
- Non-US and non-English merchants are structurally underserved: discovery is global but in-agent checkout is confined to six countries at most, so a third party could offer agent-facing eligibility declarations for the rest.
- Because Shopify disclaims channel behaviour and merchants carry all liability, small merchants bear the cost of agent errors with no Shopify-provided evidence trail — a clear wedge for a trust/verification layer.

### Gaps
- No source quantifies how many Shopify merchants are Basic vs Plus among agentic participants, or how many are headless.
- No data on how often agents hit the 7-day removal lag or stale-availability windows in practice.
- Shopify's roadmap for loyalty/member pricing in UCP, non-US direct checkout expansion, or product-level agent rules is undocumented.
