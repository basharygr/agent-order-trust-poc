Scope note for the writer: The window is May 2026 through 3 November 2026. Items from before May 2026 are included only where they are the baseline the 2026 reporting refers back to, and are marked **[BACKGROUND, <date>]**. ## Key Question 1: State of ChatGPT checkout after Instant Checkout moved "to Apps" (March 2026): which merchants are live, what volume/conversion has been reported, what merchants say

### Takeaway
As of 3 Nov 2026 there is no OpenAI-disclosed order volume, conversion rate, or merchant count for ChatGPT checkout; the only hard conversion datapoint anywhere is Walmart's pre-pivot disclosure that in-chat Instant Checkout converted at one-third the rate of click-outs (March 2026, background). Since the March pivot, OpenAI has reported only discovery- and ads-side figures (1.2B weekly users, 100,000+ advertisers, ads in 89 countries), and merchants that stayed (Walmart, Target, Etsy) describe AI-sourced traffic as small but fast-growing, high-intent, and converting well on their own sites rather than inside ChatGPT.

OpenAI statement: "We've found that the initial version of Instant Checkout did not offer the level of flexibility that we aspire to provide." CNBC's analysts said OpenAI "underestimated how hard it would be to enable transactions," had trouble onboarding merchants, struggled to show accurate product data, and lacked multi-item carts and loyalty linking. Instant Checkout merchants named: Etsy, Walmart, Shopify. New product-discovery merchants named: Target, Sephora, Nordstrom. ### Inferences
- The absence of any OpenAI order or conversion disclosure seven months after the pivot, combined with OpenAI's October emphasis on ads metrics (advertiser counts, measurement partnerships), suggests OpenAI is monetizing shopping as a discovery/advertising surface rather than as a transaction rail.
- The Clickstream study's finding that position-1 cards take 43% of picks, plus Retailgentic's observation of missing tracking parameters, means merchants have strong ranking incentives but weak attribution — a plausible reason merchants talk about "traffic" and "high intent" rather than orders.
- The only trust-related product signals in the window are additive discovery features (try-on, Dots) and the quiet removal of autonomy (agent mode, Atlas; see KQ3), not new in-chat purchase controls.

### Gaps
- No OpenAI statement on number of merchant apps live in ChatGPT, orders completed via apps, or conversion since March 2026. Not found in OpenAI blogs, help center release notes (403 on fetch), or trade press.
- No merchant complaints specific to the post-March app model were found; the complaints on record (inaccurate item data, one-item-at-a-time, no loyalty) all predate May 2026.
- The full 39-page ReFiBuy/Clickstream report may contain checkout-completion or abandonment rates; the newsletter excerpt does not.
- Fee model (2% vs 4%) is reported inconsistently in secondary sources and was not verified.

## Key Question 3: OpenAI agent mode / Operator / Atlas — purchase completion rates, error rates, safety restrictions

### Takeaway
No completion or error rates were ever disclosed; instead, in July–August 2026 OpenAI removed both autonomous surfaces consumers could use to buy: ChatGPT agent mode was pulled without a deprecation notice (users noticed ~8 Jul–early Aug 2026; OpenAI Support confirmed 7 Sep 2026) and the Atlas browser was shut down on 9 Aug 2026 after a 9 Jul announcement. Replacement surfaces (ChatGPT Work's cloud browser, Codex, the ChatGPT desktop app's built-in browser) are framed as productivity tools, not shopping.

### Cited Findings
- (Product change, 9 Jul / 9 Aug 2026) OpenAI announced Atlas's retirement on 9 Jul 2026 with a ~30-day wind-down; Atlas "is scheduled to stop working on August 9, 2026" and will no longer receive security maintenance. Lessons are "informing a more capable browser experience inside ChatGPT"; the ChatGPT desktop app gains tabs, downloads, account logins and "agent-led browsing where available"; browser automation for logged-in sites moves to Codex. — [Digital Trends, 5 Aug 2026](https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/chatgpt-atlas-is-shutting-down-and-it-has-some-homework-left-before-you-migrate/)
- (Product change, Jul–Sep 2026) OpenAI developer-community thread (posted 8 Aug 2026): "Agent Mode was removed with no real replacement"; users report no advance notice, deprecation period, or migration guide, and that the Agent documentation was changed to say the feature is no longer available. OpenAI_Support replied 7 Sep 2026 apologizing for workflow disruption and pointing to ChatGPT Work's "separate cloud browser that can navigate interactive sites" which can pause for sign-in or user takeover. — [OpenAI Developer Community, Aug–Sep 2026](https://community.openai.com/t/agent-mode-was-removed-with-no-real-replacement/1389601); [related thread](https://community.openai.com/t/agent-mode-missing-across-all-platforms-on-pro-account/1387374)
- (Third-party summary) Users began seeing agent mode disappear around 8–16 Jul 2026 and a "no longer available" notice by early August 2026; scheduled tasks that called agent mode stopped running. — [Carly blog (secondary)](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/chatgpt-agent-mode/); [Wikipedia: ChatGPT Atlas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ChatGPT_Atlas)
- (Product change, 26 Oct 2026) ChatGPT changelog: "GPT-6.1 Terra is now available in ChatGPT Work and Codex on Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu plans," acting "across your browser, desktop, and apps." No shopping or purchase language. — [ChatGPT & Codex changelog](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/changelog)
- **[BACKGROUND, Feb 2026]** Zenity Labs "Grand Theft Atlas": a planted X comment hijacked Atlas agent mode into an unauthorized Amazon purchase shipped to the attacker's address; Zenity said the final purchase button was a "hard boundary" it did not break and the injection classifier was tuned for English. OpenAI acknowledged on 17 Feb 2026 "meaningful risks associated with prompt injection in agentic environments." — [Zenity Labs](https://labs.zenity.io/post/grand-theft-atlas); [CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/openai-chatgpt-atlas-prompt-injection-browser-agent-security-update-head-of-preparedness/)
- **[BACKGROUND, pre-shutdown]** Atlas agent mode required final user confirmation before completing any purchase and could not access saved passwords or autofill. — [MIT AI Agent Index](https://aiagentindex.mit.edu/2025/chatgpt-atlas); [Yahoo Tech review](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/chatgpt/articles/let-chatgpt-atlas-walmart-shopping-114600576.html)

### Inferences
- Removing agent mode and Atlas within a month of each other, with no public post-mortem, is the strongest "removed autonomy" signal in the window; OpenAI's prior statement that prompt injection "may never be solved" for browser agents is the most plausible stated rationale, but OpenAI did not connect the two publicly.
- OpenAI never published a purchase completion or error rate for agent mode, Operator, or Atlas, so none exists to cite.

### Gaps
- No OpenAI blog post announcing agent mode's removal was found; the record is community threads and a support reply.
- No disclosed purchase completion, error, or refund rates for agent mode/Atlas at any point.
- Whether the new ChatGPT desktop "agent-led browsing" or Work cloud browser is permitted to complete purchases, and with what confirmation steps, is not documented in sources found.
