# Google and Amazon shopping agents: production data, May 2026 to 3 November 2026

Scope note: Window is May 2026 through 3 Nov 2026. Items from before May 2026 are marked **[BACKGROUND]**. "Company claim" vs "third-party measurement" is flagged on each bullet where it matters. Several commonly cited sources (GeekWire, TheStreet, Retail Brew) returned HTTP 403 and could not be read in full; where I rely on search-snippet text only, I say so.

Headline finding across both companies: **neither Google nor Amazon has disclosed a single order count, conversion rate, order-accuracy rate, cancellation rate, return rate or dispute rate for their agentic checkout products in this window.** Google's only volume-adjacent figure is "hundreds of thousands of brands" enabled for UCP checkout. Amazon's only figures are reach (400M+ Alexa for Shopping users), interaction growth (570% YoY), and a basket-size uplift (50% more per order), none of which measure agent-completed purchases. The only numeric estimates of agent-completed order share come from one agency study (FirstPageSage) and should be treated as low-confidence.

## Key question 1: Google AI Mode / Gemini agentic checkout (UCP, Google Pay, Universal Cart): which merchants are live, what has Google said about volume or conversion, what do auto-enrolled Shopify merchants say, any wrong-order or price-mismatch incidents?

### Takeaway
Google has scaled UCP checkout to "hundreds of thousands of brands" (company claim, Sept and Oct 2026) and launched Universal Cart in the US on 15 Oct 2026, but has published no order volume, conversion, cancellation or accuracy numbers; Merchant Center checkout analytics were still "coming soon" as of 16 Sept 2026. Shopify merchants are enrolled in agentic channels by default and have complained about the lack of a global opt-out, but I found no reported wrong-order or price-mismatch incident attributable to Google's checkout.

**Product rules and controls (trust/accuracy signals)**

- Universal Cart / AP2: agents buy only when the user's stated criteria are met; "tamper-proof digital mandates" record the authorisation; guardrails are specified brands, products and spending limits (Google description, 19 May 2026) — [Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/shopping/google-shopping-cart/)
- Third-party commentary says Merchant Center's UCP Analytics reports buy clicks, purchases, cancellations and cart transfers, and that transfers are counted separately from downstream purchases so merchants must reconcile against their own orders — [Robert Hu blog](https://theroberthu.com/blog/google-universal-cart-retailers-ucp-checkout); [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/news/google-universal-cart-gemini-search/). Google's own 16 Sep post still described analytics as "coming soon," so these reports are unconfirmed.
- Comparison piece (9 Sep 2026): "none of the four platforms allows a fully autonomous purchase without in-the-moment confirmation"; Gemini/UCP listed as placing orders "with user confirmation"; roughly 30 retailers/brands had some live AI checkout integration across all platforms as of July 2026 per Taylor Sicard's AI Checkout Tracker — [Tech-Insider](https://tech-insider.org/chatgpt-vs-copilot-vs-gemini-ai-shopping-agents-2026/)

### Inferences
- "Hundreds of thousands of brands" almost certainly counts Shopify merchants enabled by default rather than merchants that have individually integrated or transacted; the gap between that figure and the Merchant Center help page's "selected merchants only" early-access language supports this reading.
- Google's decision to make the merchant responsible for wrong items, cancellations and returns, and to hard-fail purchases on any price increase, is a deliberate design to avoid Google-held order liability; it also means Google has no reason to publish accuracy or dispute data.
- Google's own earnings commentary on AI Mode (lower monetization, upper-funnel, additive) signals that commercial conversion inside AI Mode is still immature as of Q3 2026, even though Google frames it positively.

### Gaps
- No Google disclosure of agentic checkout orders, GMV, conversion, cancellations or repeat use was found for I/O 2026, GML 2026, Q2 or Q3 2026 earnings.
- No independent journalist or researcher test of Google AI Mode/Gemini/Universal Cart completing real purchases with success/error counts was found (searches for Wirecutter, Verge, WSJ, WaPo style hands-on reviews returned only explainers).
- No reported wrong-order or price-mismatch incident attributable to Google's checkout was found; Shopify forum complaints concern enrollment and discoverability, not completed orders.
- The Merchant Center UCP checkout help page itself (cancellation handling, order rules) was not retrievable; only secondary descriptions were available.
- Etsy, which co-developed UCP, does not appear in any 2026 "live checkout" partner list I found; its live status is unconfirmed.

## Key question 2: Amazon Buy for Me: current scale, seller complaints with numbers, Amazon disclosure of order counts, cancellation/return rates, 2026 policy changes

### Takeaway
Amazon's last public scale figures for Shop Direct/Buy for Me are from 11 Mar 2026 (400,000+ merchants, 100M+ products, "tens of millions" Buy for Me-eligible); nothing newer was found in the May-Nov 2026 window, Amazon has never disclosed order counts, and the programme remains opt-out by email. Seller complaint numbers are all from Jan 2026 coverage (background).

### Cited Findings
- **[BACKGROUND, 11 Mar 2026]** Amazon expanded Shop Direct to accept merchant product feeds via Feedonomics, Salsify and CedCommerce; a self-service merchant portal and direct feed "coming soon"; Shop Direct "now includes over 100 million products from more than 400,000 merchants," with "tens of millions" eligible for Buy for Me; Amazon said it has sent "millions of referrals" to outside stores — [About Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-shop-direct-external-stores); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/11/amazon-expands-a-program-that-lets-customers-shop-from-other-retailers-sites/); [Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/03/11/amazon-opens-up-new-ai-enabled-buy-for-me-shop-direct-options-for-merchants/); [Retailgentic](https://www.retailgentic.com/p/alert-amazon-expands-shop-directbuy)
- **13 May 2026:** Rufus renamed Alexa for Shopping for US customers; secondary sources say Shop Direct and Buy for Me are surfaced within it — [Axios](https://axios.com/2026/05/13/amazon-alexa-ai-shopping-assistant); [Presenc.ai timeline](https://presenc.ai/research/amazon-buy-for-me-off-amazon-purchasing-2026)
- Buy for Me Terms of Use: "we are acting as your agent when we place orders at your direction, and that the Brand is the seller of the products to you"; Amazon's return policy and A-to-Z Guarantee do not apply; Amazon "cannot input any discount codes"; no brand loyalty points accrue; orders tracked in a "Buy for Me Orders" tab — [About Amazon (Buy for Me launch page)](https://aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-shopping-app-buy-for-me-brands); [RetailWire](https://retailwire.com/amazon-buy-for-me/). A search result referenced a 2 Sep 2026 Terms of Use revision but I could not verify it against Amazon's pages.
- Opt-out: by emailing branddirect@amazon.com (per Modern Retail / Value Added Resource, Jan 2026) or shopdirect@amazon.com (per Amazon merchant FAQ as quoted in 2026 vendor guides); Amazon says removals are prompt and "no new fee, no new setting" — [Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/brands-are-upset-that-buy-for-me-is-featuring-their-products-on-amazon-without-permission/); [Presenc.ai](https://presenc.ai/research/amazon-buy-for-me-off-amazon-purchasing-2026). Both addresses appear in sources; which is current is unresolved.
- **[BACKGROUND, 6 Jan 2026] Seller complaints with numbers (Modern Retail):** Angie Chua (Bobo Design Studio) collected 145 survey responses from brands who believe they were listed without consent; Mochi Kids received about 16 Buy for Me orders since Nov 2025 and is cancelling all of them; Chua received "at least several" orders for out-of-stock or deleted products and issued refunds; wrong stock image (pants) on a sticker listing; "shell" listings remained after takedowns; Amazon: "programs we're testing," "received positive feedback," does not generate AI images of brand products, "may modify" merchant titles/descriptions — [Modern Retail, 6 Jan 2026](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/brands-are-upset-that-buy-for-me-is-featuring-their-products-on-amazon-without-permission/)
- **[BACKGROUND, 5-10 Jan 2026] (Value Added Resource):** one Shopify merchant reported 8 Buy for Me orders in a month, about half of whom called asking for tracking; another merchant reported a 28% chargeback rate on Amazon-originating orders across "a dozen or so" orders before blocking them (total exposure under $10K); orders arrive from anonymised @buyforme.amazon addresses so order/tracking emails and return labels don't reach the customer; orders authorised for out-of-stock items — [Value Added Resource](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/amazon-buy-for-me-small-business-backlash/); [ChannelX](https://channelx.world/2026/01/complaints-amazon-buy-for-me-sourcing-from-retailer-websites/)
- GeekWire covered independent-brand complaints in 2026 but the page returned 403; headline only — [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/why-some-independent-brands-are-upset-with-amazons-new-buy-for-me-shopping-tool/)
- Third-party estimate (low confidence): FirstPageSage says merchants participating in Buy for Me "completed purchases at 2.3x the rate of non-participating merchants" and puts Amazon (Alexa checkout plus Buy for Me) at 30.4% of agent-completed purchases in its panel with a $53 average agent-completed order; it attributes a "May 2026" Buy for Me merchant partnership that I could not corroborate from Amazon — [FirstPageSage, 2 Nov 2026](https://firstpagesage.com/seo-blog/agentic-commerce-statistics)
- Q3 2026 earnings call: Buy for Me and Shop Direct were not mentioned — [Motley Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/30/amazon-amzn-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)

### Inferences
- With no new scale figures since March and no earnings-call mention in Q2 or Q3, Buy for Me appears to have been folded into the Alexa for Shopping narrative rather than scaled as a headline product.
- Amazon's agent-not-seller structure (no A-to-Z, no returns policy, anonymised emails) shifts fulfilment and dispute burden to merchants who never opted in, which is the structural source of the chargeback and tracking complaints.

### Gaps
- No Amazon disclosure of Buy for Me order counts, cancellation, return or chargeback rates exists in any source found.
- No seller complaint data newer than Jan 2026 with numbers was found; no confirmed change from opt-out to opt-in in 2026.
- The reported 2 Sep 2026 Buy for Me Terms of Use revision is unverified.
- Seller Central forum threads on Buy for Me in the window were not retrievable.

## Key question 3: Rufus and Alexa+ / Alexa for Shopping: Amazon's disclosed numbers, accuracy or error data, Q2/Q3 2026 earnings statements on agentic shopping

### Takeaway
Amazon's disclosed metrics in the window are all reach and engagement (350M+ users and 5x interactions at Q2; 400M+ users, 570% interaction growth, 50% higher order value at Q3; agentic feature use up 8x since start of 2026); Amazon has never disclosed how many purchases the assistant completes, and Jassy said in Q3 that "few customers are doing much shopping" through external agents yet.

### Inferences
- The jump from 350M (Q2) to 400M+ (Q3) trailing-12-month users and from 5x to 6.7x interaction growth is consistent with Alexa for Shopping being injected into the default search bar in May 2026, so the figures measure exposure more than deliberate agentic use.
- "50% more per order" is a correlation among users who choose to engage the assistant; Amazon presents no causal or purchase-completion data.
- Jassy's "few customers are doing much shopping this way yet" is the clearest company statement in the window that agent-completed commerce (via third-party agents) is not yet material.

## Product changes in the window that signal accuracy or trust concerns (consolidated)

### Takeaway
Both companies added control and confirmation mechanics rather than removing autonomy: Google launched with hard price ceilings, merchant-set transaction limits, merchant-of-record liability and per-order confirmation; Amazon added review-before-confirm actions and kept blocking external agents while fighting the Perplexity ruling.

### Cited Findings
- Google Pay agentic checkout: buys only if final price is equal to or lower than the confirmed total; fails on price increase or stock-out with no charge; merchants set min/max transaction amounts; wrong item/returns/cancellations go to the merchant — [Google Pay Help](https://support.google.com/googlepay/answer/16421147?hl=en-GB)
- Google Universal Cart (15 Oct 2026): merchant stays seller of record and handles confirmations and support; option to transfer cart to merchant site instead of Google checkout — [Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/shopping/universal-cart-launch/)
- Google UCP integration hub (16 Sep 2026): cart transfer toggle and "enhanced checkout flow testing"; analytics "coming soon" — [Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/shopping/google-shopping-updates-holiday-shopping/)
- Shopify: granular per-channel controls (catalog access, auto-enrollment, direct checkout) but no global opt-out and no opt-out from the Shop channel; merchant complaints Jun 2026 — [Shopify Community](https://community.shopify.com/t/why-cant-we-opt-out-of-agentic-storefronts/631140); [PageFly](https://pagefly.io/blogs/shopify/universal-commerce-protocol)
- Amazon Alexa for Shopping 100+ actions (29 Oct 2026): purchase-related requests require review before confirming; Auto-Buy still completes purchases automatically — [TechJournal](https://techjournal.org/alexa-shopping-100-actions)
- Amazon blocked Meta Muse (20 Sep 2026) and, per newsletter reporting, Google, OpenAI and Perplexity agents; Ninth Circuit vacated Amazon's Perplexity injunction (4 Aug 2026) — [Retail Gazette](https://www.retailgazette.co.uk/blog/2026/09/amazon-blocks-metas-new-ai-shopping-agent-from-its-website/); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2230471/perplexity-has-successfully-overturned-amazon-injunction-on-its-ai-shopping-bot/); [Shopifreaks](https://www.shopifreaks.com/amazon-ceo-andy-jassy-says-alexa-for-shopping-will-be-the-main-agent-for-shopping-amazon-even-as-it-blocks-rival-ai-agents/)
- Amazon Buy for Me: no A-to-Z Guarantee, no Amazon returns policy, no discount codes; opt-out by email only — [About Amazon](https://aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-shopping-app-buy-for-me-brands); [Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/brands-are-upset-that-buy-for-me-is-featuring-their-products-on-amazon-without-permission/)
- Context: OpenAI discontinued Instant Checkout on 4 Mar 2026 (background, competitor pullback) — [Tinuiti](https://tinuiti.com/blog/amazon/amazon-agentic-commerce/)

### Inferences
- No pulled feature or removed autonomy was found for Google or Amazon in the window; the trust signals are instead (a) refusal to publish order-level metrics, (b) liability pushed to merchants, and (c) Amazon walling off third-party agents.

### Gaps
- Whether Google changed any checkout rule (e.g., confirmation steps, price tolerance) between May and Nov 2026 could not be determined because the Merchant Center and Google Pay help changelogs were not retrievable.
