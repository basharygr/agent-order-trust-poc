# Shopify covers agents; everyone else exposes catalogs

Only Shopify ships agent enablement end to end: a default-on product-data layer (Shopify Catalog, more than one billion products) syndicated to ChatGPT, Copilot, Google AI Mode/Gemini, Meta Muse and Shop, plus UCP/MCP checkout endpoints and a Web Bot Auth-gated Checkout WebMCP for browser agents, all switched on for every Starter-or-higher store without the merchant doing anything. Everyone else exposes a catalog at best. Wix publishes a default-on UCP profile on roughly 6,700 stores but never lets an agent pay, BigCommerce turned on Cloudflare-injected WebMCP across about 100,000 domains but hands the shopper a checkout link, WooCommerce's MCP is an opt-in developer preview with no cart or checkout, and Adobe, Salesforce, commercetools, Squarespace, Webflow, Lightspeed and the regional platforms range from "committed" to absent. Across all of them, in-agent checkout exists only for buyers in the United States (Copilot, Google), Canada and Australia (Google), and at most six countries through Meta; Stripe's Agentic Commerce Suite reaches the US, Canada and unnamed European countries; nothing reaches Israel, Hebrew, ILS, or any Israeli payment processor. The data an agent sees is lossy by design on every protocol: feeds carry an availability enum but no quantity, variant attributes are optional, shipping and tax never appear as a landed number before a checkout session exists, returns policy is a URL or absent, promotions live in separate objects, and member pricing is invisible (15 of 11,414 verified UCP stores declared loyalty). Merchant controls are binary toggles; Shopify's only true opt-out is a support ticket. The browsing pattern now carries 77% of agentic orders on Forter's network, driven by Muse, yet every in-browser agent and all three leading cloud consumer agents present as ordinary Chrome, so merchants can neither allow nor block them reliably and cancel 27-37% of their orders outside North America. Quality evidence is thin and mostly vendor-sourced: no platform publishes wrong-item, mismatch or cancellation rates, the headline completion multipliers (FirstPageSage 2.7x-3.9x, Shopify's "Catalog converts 2x scraped") have no methodology, and the one study claiming Shopify stores never block agents sits in a repository that returns 404. For a team targeting small and mid-size merchants on WooCommerce, Wix, custom carts and Shopify outside the US rollout, the open ground is not catalog syndication, which Shopify and the PSPs will own, but the layer no platform ships: a per-variant, per-destination transactability verdict, rules beyond on/off, browser-agent handling at checkout, attribution that counts failed checkouts, and consent evidence for disputes.

## Shopify ships discovery plus checkout; Wix and BigCommerce ship carts; the rest ship feeds or promises

The platform-by-platform picture splits into three tiers. Shopify is alone in the top tier because its enablement is default-on at every layer. Catalog is on for any store on "the Starter plan or higher" that is not password-protected and whose products carry a title, image, price above zero and an identifiable URL ([Shopify Help Center, Catalog requirements](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-catalog/requirements)); products flow to ChatGPT, Copilot, Google and Meta under a master "Allow Shopify to manage for me" switch that also auto-enrols the store in future channels ([Shopify Help Center, Agentic storefronts home](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/agentic-home)). The timeline shows how deliberate this is: Shopify first auto-opted merchants in on 12 January 2026, made Agentic Storefronts live for all eligible merchants on 24 March, added the Agentic admin section in May, and auto-added Meta on 8 September, the day Muse launched ([Search Engine Journal, 2 Oct 2026](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/shopify-now-enrolls-your-store-in-every-new-ai-shopping-channel/591049/)). On the developer side every standard Liquid storefront serves `/agents.md`, `/llms.txt`, a `/.well-known/ucp` profile and an MCP endpoint at `/api/ucp/mcp` answering `tools/list`; a 2 November 2026 live test confirmed all of this on large standard stores and confirmed that headless storefronts return 404 for every agent file and publish no UCP profile ([DEV Community, 2 Nov 2026](https://dev.to/abhaygawade/shopify-agentsmd-and-llmstxt-what-we-found-testing-real-stores-okb)). Shopify's Checkout WebMCP, extended to checkout on 28 September 2026, registers `get_checkout`, `update_checkout` and `complete_checkout` tools for browser agents that sign with a Web Bot Auth key Shopify has registered, accepts payment only from saved Shop Pay cards, and rejects checkouts the store cannot ship to ([Shopify Dev, Checkout WebMCP](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/carts-and-checkout/checkout-webmcp)). The Fall '26 Edition (28 October 2026) added Shop Pay inside Muse, Claude Duet and Instinct, a `shop.md` skill that lets any agent search the Catalog and pay with Shop Pay, `/buy` and `/ask` UCP endpoints, and a merchant-approved knowledge base of store facts ([Shopify Editions Fall '26](https://www.shopify.com/editions/fall2026); [Shopify News, Fall '26 merchant edition](https://www.shopify.com/news/fall-26-edition-merchant)). Independent measurement matches the claim: UCP Checker's October 2026 census counted roughly **10,950 valid Shopify UCP profiles**, more than every other platform combined except Wix ([UCP Checker, State of Agentic Commerce Oct 2026](https://ucpchecker.com/blog/state-of-agentic-commerce-october-2026)).

Wix and BigCommerce form the second tier: default-on exposure that stops short of payment. Wix serves a UCP profile at `/.well-known/ucp` on every store declaring catalog search and lookup, cart, checkout, fulfillment and discount capabilities over REST and MCP on `wixapis.com`, but the profile's `payment_handlers` array is empty, so an agent that builds a checkout receives `requires_escalation` and a `continue_url` to the Wix checkout page; agents can read titles, descriptions, variants, prices, stock status and images and get a priced checkout with shipping, but cannot pay, look up orders or verify signatures, and the profile still declares the 2026-04-08 spec version against a current 2026-08-25 ([UCP Checker, Wix guide, 31 Oct 2026](https://ucpchecker.com/blog/wix-ucp-guide-ai-agent-commerce)). Wix's own marketing does not mention UCP at all; it positions Stripe ACS (automatic for "eligible" merchants on a gradual basis), PayPal Store Sync for Perplexity discoverability, and an "AI Visibility Overview" that reports citation frequency and sentiment, not orders ([Wix blog, Agentic commerce on Wix, updated 26 Oct 2026](https://www.wix.com/blog/agentic-commerce-on-wix)). BigCommerce made its MCP available to all stores at Commerce Live on 29 April 2026 ([Yahoo Finance, 29 Apr 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/bigcommerce-unveils-agentic-commerce-push-215019748.html)) and on 21 October 2026 turned on Cloudflare edge-injected WebMCP for eligible merchants by default, letting browser agents search live products, prices and stock, add to cart and receive a checkout link that the shopper clicks personally; merchants get an off-switch in a settings menu covering "all of Commerce's AI connections" ([Commerce.com press release, 21 Oct 2026](https://www.commerce.com/press/commerce-and-cloudflare-bring-ai-shopping-assistants-to-bigcommerce-merchants-online-stores-with-webmcp/); [Cloudflare blog, 21 Oct 2026](https://blog.cloudflare.com/webmcp-commerce/)). Its Google UCP checkout is early access "early Q4 2026, subject to Google approval," requires a Merchant Center account with current feeds including shipping and returns policy, and is live only for select US merchants ([BigCommerce, Google agentic checkout](https://www.bigcommerce.com/articles/ecommerce/google-agentic-checkout/)). The census found 65 verified BigCommerce stores ([UCP Checker census](https://ucpchecker.com/blog/state-of-agentic-commerce-october-2026)).

The third tier is everyone the founding team cares about most. WooCommerce's MCP shipped as beta in 10.3 (21 October 2025) and gained canonical abilities in 10.9 (June 2026) covering product query/create/update/delete and order query/status/notes, with no variant or inventory abilities, no cart and no checkout, and it is off until the merchant enables the `woocommerce_feature_mcp_integration_enabled` flag ([Digital Applied, WooCommerce 10.9 MCP guide](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/woocommerce-10-9-mcp-agent-ready-wordpress-store-guide)). Its agent checkout story is Stripe ACS through the official Stripe extension, announced 11 December 2025 as a waitlist arriving "in the coming months" with no agents, countries or fees named ([WooCommerce blog, 11 Dec 2025](https://woocommerce.com/posts/stripe-agentic-commerce-suite-for-woocommerce/)), plus community UCP plugins of uneven quality, one self-described as "vibe coded" ([WordPress.org UCP plugin](https://wordpress.org/plugins/?p=286134); [UCPhub for WooCommerce](https://wordpress.org/plugins/ucphub-for-woocommerce/)). The census found about **20 WooCommerce stores** with valid UCP profiles ([UCP Checker census](https://ucpchecker.com/blog/state-of-agentic-commerce-october-2026)). Squarespace is a named Stripe ACS partner with no help article, release note or merchant control documented anywhere ([Stripe newsroom, 11 Dec 2025](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/agentic-commerce-suite)). Adobe Commerce announced UCP and ACP support on 18 February 2026 with "no detailed rollout schedule" ([Digital Commerce 360, 23 Feb 2026](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/02/23/adobe-commerce-platform-agentic-ai-standards/)); its Commerce MCP is "not a customer-facing feature" ([antegma, Adobe Summit recap](https://www.antegma.com/en/blog/2026/04/22/adobe-summit-2026-adobe-commerce-recap/)) and agencies report no native UCP module, with full agent checkout requiring custom REST development ([Kinex Media](https://www.kinexmedia.com/blog/ucp-on-adobe-commerce/)). Salesforce says its UCP integration went GA in October 2026, yet UCP Checker found **zero** of roughly 21,400 Commerce Cloud storefronts publishing a valid manifest, many sitting behind edge security that returns 403 ([UCP Checker, Salesforce Commerce Cloud](https://ucpchecker.com/blog/salesforce-commerce-cloud-ucp-october)). Shopware is the one open-source European platform with a first-party plugin covering UCP catalog, cart, checkout, order and MCP plus OpenAI and Google feeds, free and at v1.4.0 as of 2 October 2026, but it carries a 1.43/5 rating with reports of shops going offline after install and a warning that "some external AI providers limit access to merchants in the United States" ([Shopware Store](https://store.shopware.com/en/swag705756117823f/agentic-commerce-beta.html)). PrestaShop has an official MCP module and nothing else ([Ecommerce Nation](https://www.ecommerce-nation.fr/prestashop-serveur-mcp-commerce-ia/)); Webflow's "agentic" repositioning is about agents editing sites, not shopping them ([CMSWire](https://www.cmswire.com/digital-experience/webflow-launches-source-agentic-workspace-for-marketers/)); Lightspeed's agents are merchant-ops tools and Ecwid has only third-party connectors ([Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/node/62377822); [Truto](https://truto.one/blog/connect-ecwid-to-chatgpt-sync-products-stock-and-catalog-data/)). Custom and headless carts fare worst of all: in May 2026 UCP Checker verified only **45 of 642 custom and headless stores (7%)** that attempted a UCP profile ([UCP Checker, May 2026](https://ucpchecker.com/blog/state-of-agentic-commerce-may-2026)).

## Direct checkout stops at six countries; everything else is a referral

The question "do they do it well enough" has to be split by what "it" is. Discovery is broadly solved on Shopify and partially on Wix and BigCommerce. Checkout inside the agent is not, and the limiting factor is the agent surface rather than the platform. Shopify's own channel pages are explicit: ChatGPT has no direct-checkout toggle at all because checkout happens on the merchant's online store opened in a ChatGPT in-app browser ([Shopify Help Center, ChatGPT](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/chatgpt)); Copilot direct checkout is shown only to US-based customers ([Shopify Help Center, Microsoft Copilot](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/microsoft)); Google's is "rolling out gradually to customers in the supported countries," meaning the US, Canada and Australia ([Shopify Help Center, Google](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/google)); Meta's covers the US, Canada, Mexico, Brazil, Japan and Singapore, up from three countries reported in early October ([Shopify Help Center, Meta](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/meta)). The ChatGPT situation deserves emphasis because the founding team may have read Shopify's March 2026 email claiming buyers "complete purchases inside ChatGPT"; OpenAI said that wording referred to discoverability, that Instant Checkout was moving to Apps, and that Shopify had no plans to build a dedicated ChatGPT app ([Modern Retail, 12 Mar 2026](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/shopify-says-purchases-are-coming-inside-chatgpt-through-agentic-storefronts-as-openai-retreats-on-instant-checkout/)). OpenAI's current docs state that external checkout is required, purchases complete on the merchant's own domain, and Instant Checkout inside ChatGPT is "only available to select marketplace partners" ([Checkout.com docs summarising OpenAI requirements](https://www.checkout.com/docs/payments/accept-payments/accept-a-payment-via-agentic-commerce/accept-a-payment-via-chatgpt)).

The same US gravity applies off Shopify. Google's Universal Cart launched 15 October 2026 US-only ([Google blog, 15 Oct 2026](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/shopping/universal-cart-launch/)), and its Merchant Center UCP docs say checkout "applies only to products sold within the United States," is available "for select merchants," and the integration hub is "gradually rolling out in the United States" with Australia and Canada "expected early next year" ([Google UCP Merchant Center tools](https://developers.google.com/merchant/ucp/guides/tools/merchant-center)). Microsoft's Copilot Checkout admits only English-language merchants selling to US buyers in USD, with store settings "Pilot only to select customers" ([Microsoft Advertising, agentic commerce](https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/solutions/technology/agentic-commerce)). PayPal Store Sync "currently supports merchants selling physical goods to US-based customers in USD" and excludes digital goods, subscriptions and non-US sales ([PayPal Store Sync docs](https://developer.paypal.com/store-sync/overview)). Stripe-managed agent checkout "is available in the US, Canada, and select European countries," the European list is unpublished, and the full set of checkout-event hooks is "in private preview in the US" ([Stripe docs, Sell products through agents](https://docs.stripe.com/agentic-commerce/sellers/use-cases/retail.md)). Adyen Agentic launched 16 June 2026 in limited availability for US enterprise merchants ([Adyen press release](https://www.adyen.com/press-and-media/adyen-agentic)). Shopify CEO-level framing that "every Shopify merchant is UCP-ready" and that agentic transactions "carry the exact economics as an online store transaction" is therefore accurate at the endpoint level and misleading at the transaction level ([Motley Fool, Shopify Q2 2026 transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/12/shopify-shop-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)); the Supplemental Terms also reserve Shopify's right to "assess a fee on transactions" for certain channels, an unresolved tension with the "no new fees" line ([Shopify, Agentic Storefronts Supplemental Terms](https://www.shopify.com/legal/terms-agentic-storefronts)).

Volume through these rails is small and undisclosed. Forrester counted roughly thirty Shopify merchants live on ChatGPT Instant Checkout by February 2026, and people inside the program told Modern Retail it "didn't drive sales"; a large specialty retailer wanted real-time inventory, coupons and loyalty before it would be "ready for prime time" ([Modern Retail, What went wrong with Instant Checkout](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/what-went-wrong-with-chatgpts-instant-checkout/)). Stripe has published no ACS merchant counts, GMV or conversion eleven months after launch ([Stripe blog, Everything we announced at Sessions 2026](https://stripe.com/blog/everything-we-announced-at-sessions-2026)). The companion report on production data covers the broader volume picture (DoorDash's "several hundred" agent orders, the networks calling agentic volume "very small"), and nothing in the platform documentation contradicts it.

## Every feed drops variants, shipping, returns and member pricing before the agent reasons

Fidelity loss is structural, not a bug any single platform can patch, because every agent surface runs the same two-layer design: a batch feed for discovery and a live endpoint for price and stock at the moment of purchase. The feed is where the agent reasons, and the feed is lossy by specification. OpenAI's ACP feed requires only nine fields (`item_id`, `title`, `description`, `url`, `brand`, `seller_name`, `image_url`, `availability`, `price`), with `availability` an enum (`in_stock`, `out_of_stock`, `pre_order`, `backorder`) and no quantity; variants (`group_id`, `variant_dict`), `sale_price`, `shipping_price`, returns fields and `target_countries` are all optional, a policy URL "does not set or override" the returns field, and the standard upload "currently targets the US" ([OpenAI commerce docs](https://developers.openai.com/commerce/llms-full.txt)). Google's UCP Merchant Center guidance calls `variant_option` only "strongly recommended," tells merchants to push Merchant API updates "to avoid checkout price mismatches," which is an admission that the feed is expected to be stale, and requires returns policy and a support contact to be set in Merchant Center rather than in product data ([Google UCP Merchant Center overview](https://developers.google.com/merchant/ucp/guides/overview/merchant-center)). Google's automatic item updates "may not work for products with frequent price or availability value changes" and the product "may be disapproved instead of updated if we detect a mismatch" ([Google Merchant Center help 12157888](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/12157888?hl=en)). Stripe's feed is the richest of the group, carrying inventory quantity, shipping per country and region with service, speed and price, promotions and regulatory warnings, but it has no returns-policy field and postal-code targeting is US-only ([Stripe docs, Catalog feed](https://docs.stripe.com/agentic-commerce/concepts/catalog-feed.md)).

Shopify's Catalog, the best-resourced data layer in the market, has the same holes. The Global Catalog MCP returns per-variant `price`, `list_price`, `availability` with a `running_low` flag, `eligible.native_checkout`, a `ships_to` filter and a seller `refund_policy` URL, but no shipping rates, no returns-policy content, no promotions, no member or B2B pricing; results are capped at 1,000 and `total_count` is an estimate ([Shopify Dev, Global Catalog MCP](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/catalog/global-catalog)). Shopify states plainly that agentic storefronts are D2C-only and that for products sold to both B2B and D2C "B2B pricing isn't shown" ([Shopify Help Center, Agentic storefronts: products](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/products)). On 24 September 2026 four stores returned identical UCP profiles with nine capabilities and no loyalty extension, so "agents can't see member status or member pricing" ([Shopify Community thread 675870](https://community.shopify.com/t/shopify-just-released-an-ai-shopping-agent-that-shops-your-store-through-ucp-has-anyone-tested-whether-it-can-actually-check-out-on-theirs/675870)); across the whole UCP population only **15 of 11,414 verified stores** declared identity linking and none declared payment in July 2026 ([UCP Checker, July 2026](https://ucpchecker.com/blog/state-of-agentic-commerce-july-2026)). Freshness is asserted as "real-time" with "no contractual freshness SLA," Shopify-generated enrichment and cross-store clustering cannot be turned off, and a worked example grouped Olaplex 3.4 oz and 8.5 oz sizes with very different prices under one Universal Product ID ([Retailgentic, Part 2, 25 Aug 2026](https://www.retailgentic.com/p/shopify-and-agentic-commerce-part-844)). Turning Catalog access off "can take up to 7 days" to stop data sharing ([Shopify Help Center, ChatGPT](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/chatgpt)).

Regional eligibility is the sharpest loss for a non-US merchant. Before a checkout session exists, an agent can see at most a country-level `target_countries` or `ships_to` signal; true deliverability only surfaces as an `address_undeliverable` error after a UCP checkout is created, and Checkout WebMCP confirms that "the store must ship to the address, or checkout rejects it" at checkout time, not before ([ucp.dev Checkout spec](https://ucp.dev/specification/shopping/checkout/); [Shopify Dev, Checkout WebMCP](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/carts-and-checkout/checkout-webmcp)). Landed cost is never a feed number: Google's agent-on-merchant-site checkout tracks item price only, "shipping and tax costs are not part of price tracking and will be added on top," and it "doesn't support promo codes or loyalty rewards" ([Google Pay help 16421147](https://support.google.com/googlepay/answer/16421147?hl=en)). Product types are excluded rather than modelled: Google's UCP checkout bars subscriptions, personalized goods, pre-orders, bundled services, age-restricted items and more ([Google UCP Merchant Center overview](https://developers.google.com/merchant/ucp/guides/overview/merchant-center)), and Shopify's direct checkout bars subscription-only, bundles, customizable, B2B-only, local delivery and pickup, and sign-in-required products on every channel ([Shopify Help Center, Meta](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/meta)). The one place the merchant's truth survives intact is the live UCP endpoint, where "Platforms MUST NOT substitute their own computed totals for the business's values" and must not autonomously complete a checkout with mismatched totals ([ucp.dev Checkout spec](https://ucp.dev/specification/shopping/checkout/)). The accuracy-trajectory report identified stale merchant data as the actual cause of OpenAI's Instant Checkout retreat; the schemas above show why that cause persists on every protocol.

| Data element | What the merchant holds | What the agent sees before checkout | Where it is recovered |
|---|---|---|---|
| Variants | SKU-level price, stock, options | Optional in ACP (`variant_dict`) and Google (`variant_option` recommended); Shopify Catalog returns variants but clustering can mis-group sizes | Live UCP/ACP session; "variant mismatch" is about half of failed sessions |
| Real-time stock | Quantity per location | Enum only (ACP, Google); Shopify `availability` plus `running_low`; Stripe feed carries quantity | Checkout `out_of_stock` error |
| Shipping and regional eligibility | Zones, rates, carriers, exclusions | `target_countries` (ACP), Merchant Center country targeting, Shopify `ships_to` filter; Stripe per-country/region service and price | UCP `address_undeliverable` after session; WebMCP rejects at checkout |
| Returns policy | Window, cost, conditions | URL only (Shopify `refund_policy`), optional fields (ACP), absent (Stripe); Google requires it in Merchant Center, not in feed | Shopify knowledge base store facts (Fall '26); Google checkout screen |
| Promotions | Automatic and code discounts | Separate ACP Promotions API; Stripe promo feed; Shopify compare-at only; Google Pay agentic checkout rejects promo codes | Shopify direct checkout applies automatic discounts and Functions |
| Member and B2B pricing | Customer-group prices | Never shown (Shopify); UCP Loyalty extension declared by ~0.1% of stores | Not recovered |
| Landed cost (tax, fees) | Computed at checkout | Not in any feed; Google adds shipping and tax "on top" | Live checkout totals only |

## Merchant controls are on/off switches, and the real opt-out is a support ticket

Shopify's control surface is the most developed and still binary. Beneath the master switch sit a global Catalog-access toggle that "doesn't remove products from Shop," an auto-enrol toggle, per-channel Catalog-access toggles for ChatGPT, Copilot and Meta, and per-channel Direct-checkout toggles for Google, Copilot and Meta; turning the master switch back on "reactivates all settings, including direct checkout" ([Shopify Help Center, Agentic storefronts home](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/agentic-home)). The only product-level exclusion is Unlisted status or the `seo.hidden` metafield, which also removes the product from sitemaps, search engines and the store's own search; `robots.txt.liquid` rules are "advisory, and blocking them doesn't stop Shopify Catalog syndication" ([Shopify Help Center, Agentic storefronts: products](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/products)). A support-mediated status "Store is opted out of sharing products with AI channels" exists but is not a self-serve control ([Shopify Help Center, Catalog requirements](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-catalog/requirements)). Merchants have noticed: an April 2026 thread titled "Not allowed to Opt-out of Generative AI 'Agentic Storefronts'" drew no staff reply and one merchant got the channel removed only by a support request ([Shopify Community thread 600746](https://community.shopify.com/t/not-allowed-to-opt-out-of-generative-ai-agentic-storefronts/600746)); a fitment-parts seller asked in June how to make only selected products discoverable without hiding them from Google and was never answered ([Shopify Community thread 631140](https://community.shopify.com/t/why-cant-we-opt-out-of-agentic-storefronts/631140)). No price caps, quantity caps, per-market rules, per-channel product rules or agent allowlists exist on the merchant side; Web Bot Auth key registration is a platform requirement for Checkout WebMCP, not a merchant setting ([Shopify Dev, Checkout WebMCP](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/carts-and-checkout/checkout-webmcp)). An agency claims the Fall '26 Edition added product- and collection-level agent visibility controls ([Coalition Technologies](https://coalitiontechnologies.com/blog/shopify-editions-fall-26)); Shopify's own Fall '26 merchant post describes only the knowledge base and an eligibility view, so that claim is unverified ([Shopify News, Fall '26 merchant edition](https://www.shopify.com/news/fall-26-edition-merchant)).

Elsewhere the controls are thinner or absent. Stripe lets merchants pick agents in the Dashboard and set `disable_checkout=true` to make a product discovery-only ([Stripe docs, Catalog feed](https://docs.stripe.com/agentic-commerce/concepts/catalog-feed.md)); Google's per-SKU `native_commerce(checkout_eligibility)` flag is the only SKU-level checkout gate in any protocol ([Google UCP Merchant Center overview](https://developers.google.com/merchant/ucp/guides/overview/merchant-center)); BigCommerce has a settings menu and a WebMCP opt-out enforced at the Cloudflare edge ([Cloudflare blog, 21 Oct 2026](https://blog.cloudflare.com/webmcp-commerce/)); Shopware lets the merchant choose capabilities and transports with a dry-run mode ([Shopware Store](https://store.shopware.com/en/swag705756117823f/agentic-commerce-beta.html)). Wix and WooCommerce document no agent-traffic toggles, caps or exclusions at all, and no platform surveyed documents plan-tier gating ([UCP Checker, Wix guide](https://ucpchecker.com/blog/wix-ucp-guide-ai-agent-commerce); [Digital Applied, WooCommerce MCP](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/woocommerce-10-9-mcp-agent-ready-wordpress-store-guide)). The human-in-the-loop primitive exists only in UCP: a business can answer `requires_escalation` with a `requires_buyer_review` message and a `continue_url`, and "platforms MUST NOT autonomously complete a checkout with mismatched totals" ([ucp.dev Checkout spec](https://ucp.dev/specification/shopping/checkout/)); ACP offers only `not_ready_for_payment` and a post-hoc `manual_review` order status ([OpenAI commerce docs](https://developers.openai.com/commerce/llms-full.txt)). Liability sits entirely with the merchant: Shopify's Supplemental Terms say "You remain the seller of record, and are responsible for all aspects of the transactions," disclaim any warranties for Participating Channels, and contain no clause on agent errors, chargebacks or disputes ([Shopify, Agentic Storefronts Supplemental Terms](https://www.shopify.com/legal/terms-agentic-storefronts)).

## Quality evidence: completion multipliers without methods, cancellation data from one vendor

What exists is growth and completion multiples; what does not exist anywhere is a wrong-item, price-mismatch, out-of-region or cancellation rate published by a platform. Shopify's Q2 2026 call reported AI traffic and orders each up 3x year on year, "AI searches powered by Catalog converted twice the rate of those using scraped data," AI-search conversion "nearly 80% higher than traditional organic search," and 75% of AI-attributed orders from outside its top 100 categories ([Motley Fool, Shopify Q2 2026 transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/12/shopify-shop-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)). Digital Commerce 360 noted that "AI-driven" and "agentic" are undefined, no absolute counts or dollar values are given, and nothing is third-party verified ([Digital Commerce 360, 6 Aug 2026](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/08/06/how-shopify-is-approaching-agentic-ai-2026/)); Anatta observed that the 2x figure "compares being in the feed with being scraped, not a well-filled listing with a thin one" ([Anatta](https://anatta.io/agentic-commerce/shopify/catalog-optimization/)). Shopify's Q3 2026 results, scheduled for 3 November, were not retrievable at the time of research, so any new disclosure is absent here.

The third-party numbers are no better founded. FirstPageSage's panel (13 July to 26 October 2026; 5,212 US shoppers, 46 brands, 19,850 prompts) reports purchase-completion multipliers versus non-participating merchants of 3.9x for Muse retail partners, 3.5x for Muse plus Shop Pay, 2.7x for Google UCP, 1.7x for Claude Commerce Agents and 1.4x for OpenAI feeds, with no sample size or matching method for any multiplier and no error or abandonment rates ([FirstPageSage, 2 Nov 2026](https://firstpagesage.com/seo-blog/agentic-commerce-statistics)). Forter's network data (23 July to 29 October 2026) is the only order-level outcome set: protocol agents authorize at 100% versus 98.8% for scraping agents, customer cancellations run 1-2% except 10% on Google orders, merchant cancellations are highest for ChatGPT Agent and Instinct, and merchants cancel **27% of scraping-agent orders in Asia and the Middle East and 37% in Europe** ([Forter, AI Agent Order Data, 2 Nov 2026](https://www.forter.com/blog/ai-agent-order-data/)). The production-data report treats that regional cancellation split as the single real outcome number in the market, and attributes it mainly to agents ordering outside supported geographies rather than to wrong items; the platform coverage above explains the mechanism, since no feed or storefront declares deliverability before the order exists.

Onboarding evidence points the same way. UCP Checker's playground ran 1,252 agent sessions across 105 stores and 16 models: 37.9% reached checkout, 27.4% stopped at search, 20.8% failed outright, and "variant-shape problems" accounted for about half of the failures, alongside slow tokenisation, malformed cart responses and checkout redirect loops ([UCP Checker, May 2026](https://ucpchecker.com/blog/state-of-agentic-commerce-may-2026)). In July the average UCP score among 1,183 scored stores was 56.3 out of 100 with nine A-grade stores, and 289 stores declared UCP but "don't yet hold up when you actually knock" ([UCP Checker, July 2026](https://ucpchecker.com/blog/state-of-agentic-commerce-july-2026)). Forrester's Emily Pfeiffer called Instant Checkout onboarding "an arduous process" and said "crawling and scraping is inadequate to get the full breadth of product data that you need to do a good job of commerce," while Walmart found conversion three times lower for products sold directly in ChatGPT than for click-out ([CNBC, 20 Mar 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/20/open-ai-agentic-shopping-etsy-shopify-walmart-amazon.html)). On Shopify, merchants testing the September 2026 Shopify/Anthropic reference agent reported no completed checkouts and catalogued the data failures that would produce wrong orders: two conflicting prices in markup, availability contradicting the add-to-cart button, variant-level stock hidden behind an "in stock" parent, market and currency mismatches, and UCP endpoints blocked by CDN bot rules ([Shopify Community thread 675870](https://community.shopify.com/t/shopify-just-released-an-ai-shopping-agent-that-shops-your-store-through-ucp-has-anyone-tested-whether-it-can-actually-check-out-on-theirs/675870)). A 16 October call for merchants who had seen AI-placed orders lead to returns had zero replies ([Shopify Community thread 699264](https://community.shopify.com/t/have-ai-placed-orders-led-to-unexpected-returns/699264)), and attribution is weak enough that absence of incidents proves little: two ChatGPT orders arrived with no referrer and only a `utm_source` on the landing URL, and failed agent checkouts never become orders ([Shopify Community, Would you know if an AI shopper bought from your store](https://community.shopify.com/t/would-you-know-if-an-ai-shopper-bought-from-your-store/)). Google's UCP performance report, which would expose `purchase_count`, `cancellations` and `cart_transfers` per offer and country, is alpha and "subject to change" ([Google Merchant API UCP performance reports](https://developers.google.com/merchant/api/guides/reports/ucp-performance-reports)).

## Browsing agents arrive unsigned, get blocked by accident, and fail at the door

The browsing pattern now dominates order volume and is the pattern merchants can see least. On Forter's network, screen-scraping agents went from about 1% of agentic orders in late July 2026 to **77% in the week of 19 October**, with Muse alone at 49% and buying from 74% of stores that received any agentic order; Muse placed 7.3x as many orders in its first 39 days as Google's checkout did in 13 weeks ([Forter, AI Agent Order Data](https://www.forter.com/blog/ai-agent-order-data/)). Identification is architectural: a 26 October test that sent 23 agents to a site found ChatGPT's desktop browser, Claude in Chrome, Comet in both local and cloud modes, Grok, Claude computer use and Playwright MCP all presenting stock Chrome user agents with `navigator.webdriver` false and no signature; Manus was the only agent carrying both a UA token and a Web Bot Auth signature ([dev.to, Quentin Wendegass, 27 Oct 2026](https://dev.to/quentin_wendegass/we-sent-23-ai-agents-to-our-website-most-looked-human-3a7e)). Only OpenAI's cloud browser, a subset of Google-Agent traffic and Manus sign requests; Comet "must be indistinguishable from you" by Perplexity's own design, Claude in Chrome publishes no header or opt-out, and Gemini in Chrome names no UA or IP range ([Centinel Analytica, 11 Oct 2026](https://www.centinelanalytica.com/blog/ai-browser-agents)). Muse runs a cloud Chromium that looks like desktop Chrome on Linux, sends 99.3% of requests from Cloudflare and Fastly addresses, alters its fingerprint and suppresses automation flags ([Vouched, 23 Oct 2026](https://www.vouched.id/learn/blog/muse-is-an-agent-but-it-doesnt-want-you-to-know)); DataDome found Meta quietly signing "a relatively small number" of Muse requests with Web Bot Auth from around 12 October with no published key directory ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/metas-muse-agent-gains-a-signed-id-in-a-quiet-test-datadome-finds/)). Instinct uses the user's stored credentials on a persistent cloud computer, "presents itself as a human visitor" and relays MFA codes by text ([Fingerprint, 28 Oct 2026](https://fingerprint.com/blog/how-to-detect-instinct-ai-agent/)). The standards that would fix this are early: the IETF webbotauth working group adopted its protocol draft only on 1 September 2026 ([Crawler Policy](https://crawlerpolicy.com/events/ietf-web-bot-auth-wg-officially-adopts-http-message-signatures-protocol-draft)), and Visa's Trusted Agent Protocol has processors and CDNs on the verifying side but its only live pilot is B2B ([Visa press release, 20 Oct 2026](https://usa.visa.com/about-visa/newsroom/press-releases.releaseId.22856.html)).

Blocking is therefore mostly accidental and mostly aimed at cloud agents. Billions Network's audit of 51 large US retailers found 5 explicit restrictions, 37 "incidental" blocks from legacy robots.txt rules on account and checkout paths, and "zero of 51 retailers has built a route for a verified agent to complete a purchase" ([Billions Network, 6 Oct 2026](https://billionsnetwork.substack.com/p/how-top-us-retailers-are-responding)). Akamai found commerce sites left more than 90% of AI-bot traffic in monitor-only mode ([Akamai press release](https://www.akamai.com/newsroom/press-release/akamai-research-commerce-becomes-the-epicenter-for-ai-bot-attacks-and-agentic-fraud-in-2026)), while HUMAN's measured block rate on agentic traffic rose from 8.2% in April to 21% in June 2026 and HUMAN blocks Muse by default ([HUMAN, June 2026](https://www.humansecurity.com/learn/blog/state-of-agentic-traffic-june-2026-browser-agent-tooling-for-developers-is-catching-on-fast/); [HUMAN, September 2026](https://www.humansecurity.com/learn/blog/september-2026-agentic-traffic-triples-meta-muse-debuts-at-no-1/)). Cloudflare replaced its single AI switch with Search, Agent and Training categories on all plans on 1 July 2026, blocks non-verified bots by default, and from 15 September blocks Agent traffic by default on ad-bearing pages of new domains ([Cloudflare blog, 1 Jul 2026](https://blog.cloudflare.com/content-independence-day-ai-options)). For small-merchant platforms there is no platform-wide block: Shopify's July 2025 robots.txt line about buy-for-me flows "doesn't add or remove any rules" ([Modern Retail, 14 Jul 2025](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/shopify-has-quietly-set-boundaries-for-buy-for-me-ai-bots-on-merchant-sites/)), its stricter rate limits from 30 May 2026 hit unsigned traffic hardest while Web Bot Auth-signed traffic gets higher limits ([Shopify Dev changelog, Web Bot Auth](https://shopify.dev/changelog/bots-and-agents-should-identify-themselves-via-web-bot-auth)), its Plus-only bot protection is a scheduled flash-sale tool that makes no statement about AI agents ([Shopify Help Center, bot protection](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/checkout-settings/bot-protection)), and "there's no documented way to turn off browser agents on your checkout" ([Witscode](https://witscode.com/blogs/shopify-agentic-checkout)).

Documented failures cluster at the door, not mid-form. Muse hit "an anti-bot wall that blocks automated browsers" on Amazon and stopped ([The Register, 21 Sep 2026](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/09/21/amazon-shows-metas-muse-ai-shopping-agent-the-door/5297777)); Target let it browse but "blocked autonomous clicks at checkout" ([Superpower Daily summary of CNN](https://superpowerdaily.com/posts/cnn-tries-meta-s-muse-agent-and-finds-useful-plans-but-stalled-purchases)); Walmart attributed Muse failures to human-verification checks that eject the agent ([TechCrunch, 6 Oct 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/06/the-next-hurdle-for-ai-agents-getting-websites-to-let-them-in/)). On a Shopify store Muse failed Shop Pay and fell back to Stripe Link while Instinct completed one purchase, and the tester concluded it worked "only because the store was on Shopify" ([DaVinci Commerce, 19 Oct 2026](https://davincicommerce.ai/thought-leadership/meta-muse-vs-instinct-ai-shopping-agents/)). Muse reads an accessibility-tree snapshot, "can't run its own scripts, and doesn't hover or click to reveal hidden content," and connector checkouts skip merchant pages so tags do not fire ([Botify, 21 Oct 2026](https://www.botify.com/blog/meta-muse)); that is why Shopify merchants' theme-level complaints matter, since icon-only buttons without accessible names, JS-rendered prices, cookie walls swallowing the first click and non-machine-readable variant selectors stall exactly this kind of agent ([Shopify Community thread 675870](https://community.shopify.com/t/shopify-just-released-an-ai-shopping-agent-that-shops-your-store-through-ucp-has-anyone-tested-whether-it-can-actually-check-out-on-theirs/675870)). Scraping agents' fraud approval rose from 44% to 73% only after Forter relaxed its thresholds on 24 September ([Forter, AI Agent Order Data](https://www.forter.com/blog/ai-agent-order-data/)). Agent-readable standards help the protocol layer, not the browser: llms.txt is on 78.1% of Shopify stores because Shopify pushed it by default ([Casey Burridge](https://caseyrb.com/blog/state-of-llms-txt-adoption/)), yet Pottery Barn, West Elm and Williams-Sonoma publish llms.txt and still block checkout ([Billions Network](https://billionsnetwork.substack.com/p/how-top-us-retailers-are-responding)), and no source measures whether llms.txt, JSON-LD or WebMCP raises browsing-agent completion. Shopify's Checkout WebMCP is Chromium-only, requires a registered Web Bot Auth key and saved Shop Pay cards, which excludes Muse, Instinct, Grok Bot and every unsigned in-browser agent today ([Shopify changelog, 28 Sep 2026](https://shopify.dev/changelog/webmcp-support-for-checkout)).

## Israel and non-US merchants get discovery, not checkout

The explicit finding for the team's initial market is that an Israeli merchant can be found by agents on every major platform and cannot be bought from inside any agent on any of them. On Shopify, Catalog eligibility carries no country restriction ([Shopify Help Center, Catalog requirements](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/shopify-catalog/requirements)), and the Israel marketing page says Agentic Storefronts is "enabled automatically for eligible stores" ([Shopify Israel page](https://www.shopify.com/il/agentic-storefronts)), but the ChatGPT channel requires that the store "sell to customers in the US" ([Shopify Help Center, ChatGPT](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/chatgpt)), Copilot direct checkout shows only to US buyers, Google's to US, Canadian and Australian buyers, and Meta's to buyers in six countries that do not include Israel or any European state ([Shopify Help Center, Meta](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/meta)). An Israeli Shopify store that ships internationally therefore appears in ChatGPT with a referral link and can take an in-agent order only from an American, Canadian, Australian, Mexican, Brazilian, Japanese or Singaporean buyer; its Israeli customers get nothing. On Wix, the default UCP profile presumably serves Israeli sites like any other (this was not separately tested), so an agent can price a cart and get a `continue_url`, but Wix Payments is not offered in Israel and Israeli merchants connect Isracard/PayMe, Pelecard, Tranzila, PayPlus, Grow by Meshulam or iCredit instead ([Wix support, Hebrew](https://support.wix.com/he/article/יצירת-קשר-עם-wix-payments); [Wix support, Pelecard](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-pelecard-as-a-payment-provider); [Wix support, Tranzila](https://support.wix.com/en/article/connecting-tranzila-as-a-payment-provider)), which almost certainly excludes them from Stripe ACS, whose docs say it is "intended for merchants who use Stripe as a payment service provider" ([Stripe docs, in-context selling](https://docs.stripe.com/agentic-commerce/enable-in-context-selling-on-ai-agents)). None of the Israeli processors has published support for Shared Payment Tokens, Visa TAP, Mastercard Agent Pay or AP2; Hebrew and English searches found no Globes, Calcalist or Geektime coverage of agent checkout for local merchants, and the only Israel-specific item is OpenAI opening ChatGPT self-serve advertising to Israeli companies ([Calcalist/CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/s1700wbtkmx)). Nekuda, the Israeli-founded agentic payments company, is buy-side (agent wallets and mandates), not merchant exposure ([BusinessWire, 14 May 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250514808097/en)).

The non-US picture more broadly is a discovery layer with a US checkout layer bolted on. Google's product eligibility stretches to Canada and Australia but checkout is "live for select US merchants only," buyers must be signed in, 18 or over and English-speaking ([BigCommerce, Google agentic checkout](https://www.bigcommerce.com/articles/ecommerce/google-agentic-checkout/)); Copilot is US, USD and English ([Microsoft Advertising](https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/solutions/technology/agentic-commerce)); PayPal Store Sync is US-only with a Eurozone rollout planned to start in Germany; Shopware warns that "some external AI providers limit access to merchants in the United States" ([Shopware Store](https://store.shopware.com/en/swag705756117823f/agentic-commerce-beta.html)). Europe's live signals are Visa TAP agent-executed transactions with "independent merchants" and more than 30 issuers since July 2026 ([Digital Transactions, 15 Jul 2026](https://www.digitaltransactions.net/visa-launches-trusted-agent-an-agentic-commerce-protocol/)) and ReFiBuy's partnership with Nexi aimed at European catalogs ([AziendaBanca](https://www.aziendabanca.it/notizie/tecno/nexi-refibuy-agentic-commerce)). Regional platforms are publishing UCP profiles on their own (Jumpseller 450, Shoptet 335, Tray 245, Boutir 175, Salla and Zid several hundred each but not yet valid), and Shopee's ChatGPT integration spans eight Southeast Asian and Latin American markets with redirect purchases ([UCP Checker census](https://ucpchecker.com/blog/state-of-agentic-commerce-october-2026); [Ebrun, 28 Jun 2026](https://english.ebrun.com/20260628/684949.shtml)). Meanwhile the browsing agents ignore all of these borders: ChatGPT Agent places nearly a quarter of its orders outside the US, and the result is the 27-37% merchant cancellation rate on scraping-agent orders in Asia, the Middle East and Europe ([Forter, AI Agent Order Data](https://www.forter.com/blog/ai-agent-order-data/)). For an Israeli or European SMB, then, the live agent traffic that actually arrives is unsigned browsing traffic that the merchant cannot verify, driving a checkout that was never declared agent-eligible, for destinations the agent never checked.

## Gaps a third party could fill, ranked by breadth and by how soon a platform closes them

A third-party product cannot give an Israeli merchant Google checkout, but it can make sure that whichever agent arrives, by protocol or by browser, sees a correct, declared, per-destination answer and leaves behind evidence.

Two caveats shape how to read this table. First, the third parties already circling these gaps are enterprise-priced and US-centric: Firmly Connect (Best Buy, Backcountry), Feedonomics, Forter's Agentic Commerce Engine, Adyen Agentic and PayPal all serve large US merchants and none publishes pricing ([GlobeNewswire, Firmly Connect](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/03/24/3261349/0/en/Firmly-Launches-Firmly-Connect-the-First-Agentic-Commerce-Platform-that-Allows-Merchants-to-Directly-Connect-to-Any-Agent-or-Agentic-Marketing-Channel-Without-Deploying-Any-Code.html); [Forkast, Forter](https://forkast.news/forter-just-shipped-the-most-complete-merchant-side-stack-for-ai-agent-traffic-holiday-peak-is-five-weeks-away/)), while the no-integration buyers (Rye, Crossmint) treat the storefront as a browser target and strip out merchant controls by construction ([Rye blog](https://rye.com/blog/agentic-commerce-startups); [Crossmint press release](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/crossmint-launches-agent-commerce-toolkit-133000510.html)). Second, on Shopify the catalog-syndication and checkout layers are not available to a third party at all: Catalog is default-on, cannot be replaced, and Shopify's terms let it terminate channel access "for any reason and at any time without notice" ([Shopify, Agentic Storefronts Supplemental Terms](https://www.shopify.com/legal/terms-agentic-storefronts)). What is available is everything Shopify disclaims: the verdict before checkout, the rules, the browser-agent handling, the honest attribution and the evidence after.

## Conclusion

The enablement market has already sorted itself into two layers with different owners. The syndication layer (feeds, Catalog, UCP profiles, MCP endpoints) is being absorbed by platforms and PSPs, with Shopify default-on everywhere, Wix and BigCommerce default-on for discovery and carts, and Stripe, PayPal and Google supplying the checkout rails for whoever they admit; a merchant-side product that competes there is competing with free, default-on infrastructure on the one platform that works and with waitlists everyone else will eventually clear. The transactability-and-trust layer has no owner: no platform tells an agent, before the order exists, whether this variant ships to this postcode at this landed cost with this return window, no platform lets a merchant express a rule finer than on/off, no platform can tell a verified browsing agent from an unverified one at checkout, and no platform leaves behind evidence the merchant can use when an order is disputed. The browsing pattern's rise to 77% of agentic orders in three months makes that layer the one through which most agent orders at small merchants will actually flow, because Muse, Instinct and ChatGPT Agent do not wait for a platform integration.

For the Israel-first thesis specifically, the research supports a narrower claim than "Israeli merchants are underserved": they are served identically to every non-US merchant at the discovery layer and identically excluded at the checkout layer, and the exclusion is enforced by Google, Microsoft, Meta, OpenAI, Stripe and PayPal rather than by Shopify, Wix or WooCommerce. A merchant-side product cannot lift that gate; what it can do is turn the agent traffic that arrives regardless (unsigned browsers, referral clicks from ChatGPT, Wix `continue_url` handoffs to an Israeli PSP checkout) into orders that are correct for the destination and defensible afterwards, and in doing so build the eligibility and evidence records that will be required when the rails do open. The clearest near-term risk is not that platforms close these gaps, since none has a documented plan to, but that order volume at small non-US merchants stays too low to pay for the layer before the PSPs decide to bundle a thinner version of it.
